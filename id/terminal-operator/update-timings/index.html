Update Timings

Gate times move and departures run late. Update Timings is where you record both, so the gate window used for pre-immigration scanning and the time shown to passengers stay truthful.

Current Trips → open the trip → Update Timings → Save Timings

Only while the trip is Scheduled or Boarding

Once a trip is Closed, Departed or Cancelled, its timings are locked and the Update Timings action disappears. The system rejects a late attempt with "Cannot update timings for a trip that is already …"

catatan

You need permission to view Current Trips and update trip timings. The screenshots below use https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/.

Before you start​

  • You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see Sign In to the Terminal Portal.
  • You have set your active port.
  • The trip is in Current Trips and is still Scheduled or Boarding.

Step 1 — Open the trip​

Open Operational → Current Trips (route: /current-departure-trips) and click a Scheduled or Boarding trip to open its detail page.

Step 2 — Click Update Timings​

On the detail page, the Timings card shows Gate Open / Close, Departure and — if one is set — Delayed To. Click Update Timings inside that card.

tip

The trip row's ⋯ menu in the Current Trips list also offers Update Timings, if you would rather not open the trip.

Step 3 — Enter the new timings​

FieldNotes
Gate OpenWhen the gate opens for this trip.
Gate CloseWhen the gate closes. Must be after Gate Open.
Departure TimeThe trip's original scheduled departure. This cannot be changed — it stays as the reference everyone booked against.
Mark as DelayedTick only when the departure is actually running late.
Delayed ToThe new expected departure time. Appears — and becomes required — once Mark as Delayed is ticked. Must be after the scheduled departure.

The Update Timings dialog. Tick Mark as Delayed to reveal and set Delayed To.

A delay never rewrites the scheduled departure

Ticking Mark as Delayed does not change Departure Time. The original stays put as the scheduled reference, and Delayed To becomes the effective departure that staff and passengers work to. This is deliberate — it keeps a record of how late the sailing actually was.

Step 4 — Save​

Click Save Timings.

If something is wrong, you will see one of these:

MessageWhat to fix
"Gate close time must be after gate open time."Gate Close is at or before Gate Open.
"Delayed time is required when marking as delayed."You ticked Mark as Delayed but left Delayed To empty.
"Delayed time must be after the scheduled departure."Delayed To is at or before the original departure time. A delay has to be later than the time it is delaying.

Outcome​

The detail page reloads with the new timings. If you marked the trip delayed, the Delayed To time is shown in amber next to the original departure, so anyone looking at the trip sees both the scheduled and the expected time.

The trip after saving — the delayed departure time is now shown alongside the scheduled one.

Update as often as you need

There is no limit. As long as the trip is still Scheduled or Boarding, you can adjust the gate times or push Delayed To again each time the situation changes.

What else the timings affect​

  • Pre-immigration scanning on a Scheduled trip is only allowed inside the gate window. Widening the window here is how you let a late passenger through — see Scan a Boarding Pass (Pre-Immigration). Once the trip is Boarding, the window no longer applies.
  • The last-call alert on the Boarding Scan page counts down to the effective departure — the Delayed To time when the trip is delayed, otherwise the scheduled departure. See Last Call.
  • Ferry operator check-in closes when the gate closes, not when boarding starts. Extending Gate Close reopens their check-in window.
