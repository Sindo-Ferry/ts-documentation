Update a Passenger's Status

Update Status sets a passenger's stage directly, without a scan. It is the manual override for when the record and reality have come apart — a scanner failed, a passenger was waved through during a rush, a revert was missed.

Current Trips → open the trip → passenger's ⋯ menu → Update Status → pick → Update Status

Reach for the scan first

Scanning is the normal way a passenger changes stage, and it is the one that leaves a proper trail. Use Update Status when a scan is not possible or would be wrong — not as a shortcut past the gate.

Step 1 — Find the passenger​

From within their trip:

  • Today's trip: Operational → Current Trips → click the trip → the Passengers table at the bottom.
  • An older trip: Management → Departure Trips → find the trip → open its detail page.
  • Not sure which trip? Look Up a Passenger first — it searches every trip and tells you which one they are on.

A passenger's ⋯ menu on a trip opened from Current Trips — Update Status is the fourth item.

Step 2 — Open Update Status​

On the passenger's row, click ⋯ and choose Update Status.

If it is not in the menu, the passenger is Cancelled — see Cancelled passengers below.

Step 3 — Pick the new status and confirm​

The dialog reads Update Status — "Change the status of [name] ([No])."

Open the New Status picker. It offers exactly three:

StatusMeaning
CheckedInHas a boarding pass; has not cleared pre-immigration.
PreImmigrationHas cleared pre-immigration; waiting to board.
BoardedIs on the vessel.

The Update Status dialog, with PreImmigration picked for a CheckedIn passenger.

Choose one and click Update Status (or Cancel to back out). The button stays disabled while the selected status is the one they already have, so you cannot submit a no-op.

Outcome​

  • The passenger's status changes immediately.
  • The trip's counts adjust — one count down, another up.
  • A timestamp is stamped for the new status: setting CheckedIn records a new checked-in time, PreImmigration a pre-immigration time, Boarded a boarded time. Each is set to now, not to when the passenger actually passed that point.
  • The change takes effect whatever the trip's status is — unlike the scan-page reverts, this is not blocked once the trip is Closed or Departed. That is precisely why it exists.
It moves in any direction

Unlike scanning, this is not a one-way street. You can set a Boarded passenger back to CheckedIn in a single step, or jump CheckedIn straight to Boarded. There is no ordering rule.

Update Status cannot cancel​

Two hard rules:

  1. Cancelled is not in the picker. The list is CheckedIn, PreImmigration and Boarded only. To cancel someone, use Cancel a Passenger — the action that also releases the seat. Force a Cancelled value through and the answer is "Direct status update does not support Cancelled. Use cancel action instead."
  2. A cancelled passenger cannot be updated. Update Status is not shown at all on a Cancelled row. Force it and the answer is "Cancelled passenger status cannot be updated directly." There is no route from Cancelled back to CheckedIn — check them in again instead.

Update Status vs. the scan reverts​

Both move a passenger backwards. They are not interchangeable:

Revert on the scan pageUpdate Status
WherePre-Immigration Scan / Boarding ScanThe trip's passenger table
Needs the trip to beScheduled or BoardingAny status
MovesOne step backAny of the three, in any direction
Best forFixing a scan you just made, at the gateFixing a record after the fact

At the gate, revert. At the counter, update.

If the update is refused​

MessageWhat it means
"Trip CheckInCount is inconsistent for this status transition." (or PreImmigrationCount / BoardedCount)The trip's counters do not line up with the change — usually two people editing the same trip at once. Refresh the page and try again; if it persists, report it.
"Cancelled passenger status cannot be updated directly."The passenger is cancelled.
"Direct status update does not support Cancelled."Use Cancel a Passenger.
