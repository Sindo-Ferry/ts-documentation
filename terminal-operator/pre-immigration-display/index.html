Passenger Display Setup (Pre-Immigration)

The Passenger Display is the screen that faces the passenger at the pre-immigration desk. It shows a large green Success or red Rejected. after every scan, so the passenger knows immediately whether to walk on or stop and talk to you.

Set it up once at the start of your shift and leave it running.

Pre-Immigration Scan → Open Passenger Display → drag to the second screen → F11

Step 1 — Open the display​

On the Pre-Immigration Scan page, the right-hand panel is headed Passenger Display Preview. Click Open Passenger Display in its header.

The display opens in a new browser tab, showing its idle screen.

The Passenger Display as it first opens — idle, waiting for the first scan.

Step 2 — Move it to the second screen​

Drag that tab out into its own window, then drag the window onto the monitor facing the passengers.

Step 3 — Full-screen it​

With the display window focused, press F11. The browser chrome — address bar, tabs, bookmarks — disappears and the display fills the monitor.

Press F11 again at any time to come back out.

Keep the scan tab and the display tab both open

The display is driven by the scan page in the same browser. If you close the scan tab, or open the display in a different browser or on a different computer, it will sit there showing the idle screen and never update.

Step 4 — Go back to the scan tab and work​

Click back onto your operator screen. The Boarding Pass Number field needs focus for the hardware scanner to work, so click into it before the first passenger.

You do not need to look at the second monitor while you work — the Passenger Display Preview panel on your own screen shows exactly the same thing, live.

What passengers see​

Idle — between scans​

A dark screen reading Ready For Pre-Immigration / "Waiting for next scanned passenger..." This is also what it shows when the display has just been opened — see the picture in Step 1.

Success — the passenger may proceed​

A green screen with a large tick, the word Success, the passenger's name, and their operator and departure time.

A successful scan — the passenger is cleared to Pre-Immigration.

Rejected — the passenger must stop​

A red screen with a cross, the word Rejected., and a short message.

A rejected scan — the passenger is not cleared.

The display never shows internal detail

Passengers see only two kinds of real reason — gate window messages ("Pre-immigration gate is not open yet…") and "Invalid boarding pass number."

Every other rejection — wrong status, wrong port, a configuration problem — appears on the public screen as "Please Check with our terminal staff". The real reason is shown on your screen only. Nobody's travel status is put on a large public monitor.

Back to idle​

About 10 seconds after each scan the result fades and the display returns to Ready For Pre-Immigration.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomCauseFix
The display stays on Ready For Pre-Immigration while you scanThe scan tab was closed, or the display was opened from a different browser / machineRe-open the display from the Open Passenger Display button on the scan page, and leave both tabs open
The display is on the wrong monitorIt opened on the operator screenDrag the window across, then press F11
Browser tabs and the address bar are visible to passengersNot in full-screenClick the display window, press F11
It shows an old passenger's resultNormal — results stay up for about 10 secondsWait, or scan the next passenger
