General Support

The counter that sits beside the flow — check in NTL / LM passengers, fix passport details, cancel a passenger, correct a status, reprint a boarding pass, download the manifest, and export reports.

📄️Look Up a Passenger

Find any passenger's record across past, today's and future trips from the read-only Passengers list, and open their full details.

📄️Check In NTL/LM

Manually check a Not-To-Land or Last-Minute passenger onto a trip from the trip's detail page, with all the validation rules.

📄️Edit CheckedIn Passenger Detail

Correct a passenger's passport details from the trip's passenger table — only while they are still Checked-In.

📄️Cancel a Passenger

Cancel a passenger from a trip, releasing their seat — what it does, when it is allowed, and what it cannot undo.

📄️Update a Passenger Status

Move a passenger directly between Checked-In, Pre-Immigration and Boarded when the scan record does not match reality.

📄️Reprint / Download Boarding Pass

Reprint a lost boarding pass or download it as a PDF from the trip's passenger table, and check who issued it before.

📄️Download Manifest

Download a trip's passenger manifest as an Excel file in English or Indonesian — who appears on it, and where the button lives.

📄️Export Other Reports

Generate the Terminal Portal's reports — the Passenger Manifest report, the Passenger Summary for a port, and the Daily Passenger Report for an operator — as PDF or Excel.
