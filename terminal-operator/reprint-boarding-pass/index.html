Reprint or Download a Boarding Pass

Passes get lost, spilt on, and left in taxis. Reprinting one is quick, and — this is the important part — it reissues the same boarding pass number. The old pass and the new one are the same pass.

Current Trips → open the trip → printer icon (or download icon) on the passenger's row

Step 1 — Find the passenger​

From within their trip:

  • Today's trip: Operational → Current Trips → click the trip → the Passengers table at the bottom.
  • An older trip: Management → Departure Trips → find the trip → open its detail page.
  • Not sure which trip? Look Up a Passenger first — it searches every trip and tells you which one they are on.

The table's BP No column shows each passenger's boarding pass number, which is a quick way to confirm you have the right row.

The printer and download icons sit just left of the ⋯ menu. Hovering the printer shows Print boarding pass.

Step 2 — Use the printer or download icon​

At the right-hand end of the passenger's row, before the ⋯ menu, are two small icon buttons:

IconWhat it does
🖨 PrinterOpens the boarding pass and sends it to print.
⬇ DownloadSaves the boarding pass as a PDF, named boarding-pass-<number>.pdf.
They are not inside the ⋯ menu

These are separate icon buttons sitting next to the ⋯ menu, not items in it. People hunt through the dropdown and conclude the feature is missing. Look to the left of the three dots.

Both icons show a spinner while the pass is being generated.

Step 3 — Check the number​

The reprinted pass carries the same boarding pass number as before. Reprinting never generates a new one. A passenger holding an old printout and a new printout has two copies of the same pass — either one scans.

The only thing that produces a new number is checking the passenger in again from scratch, which means cancelling them first.

When the icons are greyed out​

Hover for the tooltip, which names the reason:

TooltipWhat it meansWhat to do
"This passenger has no boarding pass number."No pass was ever issued for this record.Rare. Check the record was created properly; it may need to be cancelled and re-checked-in.
"This passenger is cancelled."Cancelled passengers have no valid pass.If they are travelling after all, check them in again — they get a new number.
"This trip is cancelled."The whole sailing is off.Nobody on a cancelled trip has a valid pass.
"Loading trip…"The page is still fetching the trip.Wait a moment.

If you see no icons at all, you do not hold the boarding-pass permission — ask your administrator.

Step 4 — Check the issue history (optional)​

To see how many times a pass has been issued and by whom, open the row's ⋯ menu and choose Boarding Pass History.

The dialog shows the passenger's name and boarding pass number, then three totals — Total, Printed and Downloaded — and a list of every issue. Each entry shows:

  • a printer or download icon, for how it was issued,
  • who issued it,
  • when, and
  • a badge reading Terminal or Operator, so you can tell a terminal reprint from one done at the ferry operator's own counter.

Boarding Pass History — one print at the operator's counter, one download at the terminal.

If nothing has been issued yet, it reads "No boarding pass issued yet."

This is the quickest way to answer "has this passenger already been given a pass today?" — and to see whether a colleague printed one two minutes ago.

Common situations​

SituationWhat to do
Passenger lost their passReprint. Same number; their scan will work.
The printout is smudged and the barcode will not scanReprint. Failing that, type the 16-character number into the scan field by hand — it is in the BP No column.
Passenger wants it on their phoneUse the download icon and send them the PDF.
The printed name is wrongEdit the passenger first (only possible while CheckedIn), then reprint.
Passenger insists they never got oneCheck Boarding Pass History before reprinting.
Pass was issued at the operator's counter and they want anotherReprint from here. The history will show both issues, tagged Operator and Terminal.
