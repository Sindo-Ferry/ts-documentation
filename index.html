Terminal Portal Documentation

Getting started​

New user? Both terminal staff and ferry operators must register an account first — sign up, confirm your email, and get activated by an administrator before signing in.

New to the system as a whole? Read How the Pieces Fit Together — a one-page map of how ports, operators, vessels, routes, timeslots, trips, and passengers depend on each other, and the order to set them up in.

Audiences​

  • Terminal Staff — operating the Terminal Portal (ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg): current trips, passenger check-in, departures, and more.
  • Ferry Operator — using the Operator Portal (ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg).
  • Public / Passengers — the no-login Live TV departure boards shown on terminal monitors.

Terminal Staff guides​

Common

  • Sign In to the Terminal Portal
  • Set Port Context

Administrative Staff

  • Operators — ferry operator master data
  • Vessels — manage the ferries used for trips
  • Ports — terminals, with timezone
  • Gates — boarding gates per port
  • Berths — berths per port
  • Routes — origin → destination routes
  • Countries — nationality list, popular flag & holiday scoping
  • Public Holiday — per-country holiday dates for scheduling
  • Timeslots — recurring weekly schedule templates
  • Generate Departure Trips with a Trip Sync Job
  • Departure Trips — view/manage trips & create AdHoc trips
  • Passengers — look up customer records (search-only)

Operational Staff

  • Manage Current Trip Status — Boarding → Close → Depart
  • Update Current Trip Timings — gate times & delays
  • Check In an NTL / LM Passenger
  • Pre-Immigration Scan — clear passengers to Pre-Immigration
  • Boarding Scan — board passengers at the gate
  • Passenger Manifest — download the boarded-passenger manifest

Ferry Operator guides​

  • Sign In to the Operator Portal
  • Timeslots — read-only view of your timeslots
  • Departure Trips — weekly calendar & change vessel
  • Passengers — read-only passenger list & details
  • Check In a Passenger
