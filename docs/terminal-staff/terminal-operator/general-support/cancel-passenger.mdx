Cancelling a passenger takes them off the sailing and **releases their seat**. Use
it for no-shows, duplicated records, and passengers who change their plans at the
counter.

> **Current Trips → open the trip → passenger's ⋯ menu → Cancel Passenger → Confirm Cancel**

:::warning[This cannot be undone]
There is no "un-cancel". A cancelled passenger stays cancelled; to put them back on
the trip you must [check them in again](/terminal-operator/check-in-ntl-lm), which
issues a **new boarding pass number** and makes their old printed pass useless.
:::

## Step 1 — Find the passenger

Passengers are cancelled **from within their trip**:

- **Today's trip:** **Operational → Current Trips** → click the trip → the
  **Passengers** table at the bottom.
- **An older trip:** **Management → Departure Trips** → find the trip → open its
  detail page.
- **Not sure which trip?** [Look Up a Passenger](/terminal-operator/look-up-passenger)
  first — it searches every trip and tells you which one they are on.

Check the **No** and the **Name** against whoever is standing in front of you before
you go near the menu.

## Step 2 — Cancel

On the passenger's row, click **⋯** and choose **Cancel Passenger** (in red, at the
bottom of the menu).

A confirmation appears:

> **Cancel Passenger** — This will cancel passenger **_[name]_** (_[No]_). This
> cannot be undone.

Click **Confirm Cancel**, or **Keep Passenger** to back out.

## Outcome

- The passenger's status becomes **Cancelled**.
- **The seat is released** — the trip's **Available** count goes up by one, so
  someone else can be checked in.
- Whichever count they were in drops by one: **Checked-In**, **Pre-Immigration** or
  **Boarded**.
- **Who cancelled them, and when, is recorded** against the passenger and visible on
  their View Passenger page.
- Their **boarding pass icons become disabled**, with the tooltip _"This passenger
  is cancelled."_
- They are **left off the manifest**, which lists Boarded passengers only.

## Who can be cancelled

Any passenger who is not **already cancelled** — **CheckedIn**,
**PreImmigration** or **Boarded**. Cancelling someone who has already boarded is
allowed; it is how you handle a passenger who comes back off the vessel.

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Passenger is CheckedIn / PreImmigration / Boarded | Cancelled, seat released |
| Passenger is already **Cancelled** | **Cancel Passenger** is **not in the menu** at all. Force it through and the answer is _"Passenger is already cancelled."_ |

:::info[The menu hides itself]
On a Cancelled row, both **Cancel Passenger** and **Update Status** disappear from
the **⋯** menu. Only **View Passenger** and **Boarding Pass History** remain. So if
you cannot find the cancel action, the passenger is probably already cancelled —
check the status badge.
:::

## Cancelling frees the document number too

The duplicate check — _"A passenger with this No already exists on this trip."_ —
**ignores cancelled passengers**. So the standard fix for a mis-keyed check-in is:

1. **Cancel** the wrong record.
2. **Check in** again with the correct details and the same document number.

It works, and it is the intended route when the record is too far gone to
[edit](/terminal-operator/edit-passenger).

## Cancel vs. Update Status

These are separate actions and it matters which you reach for:

| | **Cancel Passenger** | **Update Status** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is for | The passenger is **not travelling** | The passenger **is** travelling, but their recorded stage is wrong |
| Releases the seat | **Yes** | No |
| Can set Cancelled | **Yes** — this is the only way | **No** — Cancelled is not in the list |
| Reversible | No | Yes, by setting the status back |

You **cannot** cancel a passenger through Update Status. The status picker offers
CheckedIn, PreImmigration and Boarded only, and the system rejects a Cancelled value
with _"Direct status update does not support Cancelled. Use cancel action instead."_
See [Update a Passenger's Status](/terminal-operator/update-passenger-status).

## Cancelling a passenger vs. cancelling a trip

Cancelling every passenger one by one is **not** how you cancel a sailing. To take
the whole trip off, see
[Cancel and Restore a Trip](/terminal-operator/cancel-restore-trip) — and note that a
trip **cannot be cancelled once it has checked-in passengers**, so on a trip with
bookings the passengers do have to be dealt with first.
