Most passengers are checked in by the **ferry operator** at their own counter. Two
kinds are not, and those are yours:

- **NTL — Not To Land:** a passenger who stays on board and does not land at the
  destination.
- **LM — Last Minute:** a walk-up added right before departure.

> **Current Trips → open the trip → Check In NTL / LM Passenger → fill in → Check In Passenger**

:::info[Terminal staff can only check in NTL and LM]
The check-in dialog in the Terminal Portal offers **NotToLand** and **LastMinute**
and nothing else. Ordinary passengers go through the operator's own portal — see
[Check In a Passenger](/ferry-operator/check-in-passenger).
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context).
- The trip is **Scheduled** or **Boarding**, and has at least one free seat.
- You have the passenger's **travel document** in hand. Every field below except
  Issue Date is required.

## Step 1 — Open Current Trips

In the sidebar, open **Operational → Current Trips**
(route: `/current-departure-trips`). This lists **today's** trips with their vessel,
destination, departure time, status and live counts.

![Current Departure Trips list](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/01-current-trips.png)
_The Current Departure Trips list._

## Step 2 — Open the trip

Click the trip's row. The detail page opens, showing **Trip Info**, **Timings** and
a **Passengers** section with live counts — **Checked-In**, **Pre-Immigration**,
**Boarded** and **Available**.

Glance at **Available** before you start. Zero means the vessel is full and the
check-in will be rejected.

![Trip detail page with the Check In NTL / LM Passenger button](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/02-trip-detail.png)
_The trip detail page. **Check In NTL / LM Passenger** sits above the passenger table._

## Step 3 — Click Check In NTL / LM Passenger

The button sits in the toolbar above the passenger table. A dialog opens with the
**Departure Trip** already filled in and locked to this trip — you cannot check
someone onto the wrong sailing from here.

![Check In NTL / LM Passenger dialog with the Passenger Type options](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/03-check-in-dialog.png)
_The dialog. **Passenger Type** chooses between **NotToLand** and **LastMinute**._

:::warning[The button is missing?]
**Check In NTL / LM Passenger** only appears while the trip is **Scheduled** or
**Boarding**. On a **Closed**, **Departed** or **Cancelled** trip it is not shown at
all. That is the answer — the trip is past check-in.
:::

## Step 4 — Fill in the passenger

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **No** | Passport / ID number. **Letters and digits only** — no dashes, slashes or spaces. At least 3 characters. |
| **Name** | Full name. **Letters, digits and spaces only.** At least 2 characters. |
| **Gender** | Male, Female or Unspecified. Defaults to **Unspecified**. |
| **Passenger Type** | **NotToLand** or **LastMinute**. Defaults to NotToLand — change it for a walk-up. |
| **Nationality** | Pick from the country list. **Picking a nationality auto-fills Issue Country with the same country** — change it if the document was issued elsewhere. |
| **Date of Birth** | Must be in the past; future dates are not selectable. |
| **Issue Date** _(optional)_ | The only optional field. Cannot be in the future. |
| **Expiry Date** | **Required.** Cannot be in the past — the earliest selectable date is today. |
| **Issue Country** | Auto-filled from Nationality; change it if needed. |

Then click **Check In Passenger**.

## Step 5 — Print the boarding pass

If you hold the boarding-pass permission, a prompt appears as soon as the passenger
is created:

> **Passenger Checked In** — Print the boarding pass now?

![The Passenger Checked In prompt offering to print the boarding pass](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/04-confirm-print-bp.png)
_The print prompt, with the new passenger already in the table behind it._

Click **Print Boarding Pass** to send it straight to the printer, or **Not Now** to
skip it. You can always print it later from the passenger's row — see
[Reprint or Download a Boarding Pass](/terminal-operator/reprint-boarding-pass).

## Outcome

- The passenger appears in the trip's table with status **CheckedIn**.
- A unique **16-character boarding pass number** is issued. This is what gets
  scanned at [pre-immigration](/terminal-operator/pre-immigration-scan) and the
  [boarding gate](/terminal-operator/boarding-scan).
- The trip's **Checked-In** count goes up and **Available** goes down by one.

## If the check-in is rejected

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _"No must contain only letters and digits. Invalid character(s): '-', '/'"_ | The document number has symbols in it. The message **names the offending characters**. | Strip them out — `A-12/3` becomes `A123`. |
| _"Name must contain only letters, digits, and spaces. Invalid character(s): …"_ | The name has punctuation in it. | Remove apostrophes, hyphens and accents. |
| _"A passenger with this No already exists on this trip."_ | Someone with that document number is already on this sailing. | Check the passenger table — it may be a genuine duplicate. **Cancelled passengers are ignored by this check**, so if the earlier record was cancelled you can re-use the number. |
| _"No available seats on this trip."_ | The vessel is full. | Put them on another sailing, or [cancel](/terminal-operator/cancel-passenger) a no-show to free a seat. |
| _"Cannot add a passenger to a trip that is already Closed."_ (or Departed) | The trip has moved past check-in. | Another sailing. |
| _"Cannot add a passenger to a cancelled trip."_ | The trip is cancelled. | Another sailing. |
| _"Issue date must be before the expiry date."_ | Issue Date is on or after Expiry Date. | Re-read the document. |
| _"Date of birth must be before the issue date."_ | Date of Birth is on or after Issue Date. | Re-read the document. |
| A field is flagged on its own | One of the required fields is empty. | Every field except **Issue Date** is required. |

:::tip[Duplicate check ignores cancelled passengers]
If you check a passenger in, cancel them, then check in someone with the same
document number, it is **allowed**. Only live passengers count towards the duplicate
rule — which is exactly what you want when correcting a mis-keyed record.
:::
