**Update Status** sets a passenger's stage directly, without a scan. It is the
manual override for when the record and reality have come apart — a scanner failed,
a passenger was waved through during a rush, a revert was missed.

> **Current Trips → open the trip → passenger's ⋯ menu → Update Status → pick → Update Status**

:::warning[Reach for the scan first]
Scanning is the normal way a passenger changes stage, and it is the one that leaves
a proper trail. Use **Update Status** when a scan is not possible or would be wrong
— not as a shortcut past the gate.
:::

## Step 1 — Find the passenger

From within their trip:

- **Today's trip:** **Operational → Current Trips** → click the trip → the
  **Passengers** table at the bottom.
- **An older trip:** **Management → Departure Trips** → find the trip → open its
  detail page.
- **Not sure which trip?** [Look Up a Passenger](/terminal-operator/look-up-passenger)
  first — it searches every trip and tells you which one they are on.

![The passenger row menu with Update Status](/img/terminal-staff/edit-passenger/02-edit-from-current-trip.png)
_A passenger's **⋯** menu on a trip opened from **Current Trips** — **Update Status**
is the fourth item._

## Step 2 — Open Update Status

On the passenger's row, click **⋯** and choose **Update Status**.

If it is not in the menu, the passenger is **Cancelled** — see
[Cancelled passengers](#update-status-cannot-cancel) below.

## Step 3 — Pick the new status and confirm

The dialog reads **Update Status** — _"Change the status of **[name]** ([No])."_

Open the **New Status** picker. It offers exactly three:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **CheckedIn** | Has a boarding pass; has not cleared pre-immigration. |
| **PreImmigration** | Has cleared pre-immigration; waiting to board. |
| **Boarded** | Is on the vessel. |

![The Update Status dialog](/img/terminal-staff/edit-passenger/05-update-status.png)
_The Update Status dialog, with **PreImmigration** picked for a CheckedIn passenger._

Choose one and click **Update Status** (or **Cancel** to back out). The button stays
**disabled while the selected status is the one they already have**, so you cannot
submit a no-op.

## Outcome

- The passenger's status changes immediately.
- The trip's counts adjust — one count down, another up.
- A **timestamp is stamped for the new status**: setting **CheckedIn** records a new
  checked-in time, **PreImmigration** a pre-immigration time, **Boarded** a boarded
  time. Each is set to **now**, not to when the passenger actually passed that point.
- The change takes effect **whatever the trip's status is** — unlike the scan-page
  reverts, this is not blocked once the trip is Closed or Departed. That is
  precisely why it exists.

:::info[It moves in any direction]
Unlike scanning, this is not a one-way street. You can set a Boarded passenger back
to CheckedIn in a single step, or jump CheckedIn straight to Boarded. There is no
ordering rule.
:::

## Update Status cannot cancel

Two hard rules:

1. **Cancelled is not in the picker.** The list is CheckedIn, PreImmigration and
   Boarded only. To cancel someone, use
   [Cancel a Passenger](/terminal-operator/cancel-passenger) — the action that also
   releases the seat. Force a Cancelled value through and the answer is _"Direct
   status update does not support Cancelled. Use cancel action instead."_
2. **A cancelled passenger cannot be updated.** **Update Status** is **not shown at
   all** on a Cancelled row. Force it and the answer is _"Cancelled passenger status
   cannot be updated directly."_ There is no route from Cancelled back to
   CheckedIn — [check them in again](/terminal-operator/check-in-ntl-lm) instead.

## Update Status vs. the scan reverts

Both move a passenger backwards. They are not interchangeable:

| | **Revert on the scan page** | **Update Status** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | [Pre-Immigration Scan](/terminal-operator/revert-pre-immigration) / [Boarding Scan](/terminal-operator/revert-boarding) | The trip's passenger table |
| Needs the trip to be | Scheduled or Boarding | **Any status** |
| Moves | One step back | **Any** of the three, in any direction |
| Best for | Fixing a scan you just made, at the gate | Fixing a record after the fact |

At the gate, revert. At the counter, update.

## If the update is refused

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| _"Trip CheckInCount is inconsistent for this status transition."_ (or PreImmigrationCount / BoardedCount) | The trip's counters do not line up with the change — usually two people editing the same trip at once. Refresh the page and try again; if it persists, report it. |
| _"Cancelled passenger status cannot be updated directly."_ | The passenger is cancelled. |
| _"Direct status update does not support Cancelled."_ | Use [Cancel a Passenger](/terminal-operator/cancel-passenger). |
