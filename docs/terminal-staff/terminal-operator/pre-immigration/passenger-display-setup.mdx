The **Passenger Display** is the screen that **faces the passenger** at the
pre-immigration desk. It shows a large green **Success** or red **Rejected.** after
every scan, so the passenger knows immediately whether to walk on or stop and talk
to you.

Set it up once at the start of your shift and leave it running.

> **Pre-Immigration Scan → Open Passenger Display → drag to the second screen → F11**

## Step 1 — Open the display

On the **Pre-Immigration Scan** page, the right-hand panel is headed **Passenger
Display Preview**. Click **Open Passenger Display** in its header.

The display opens in a **new browser tab**.

## Step 2 — Move it to the second screen

Drag that tab out into its own window, then drag the window onto the **monitor
facing the passengers**.

## Step 3 — Full-screen it

With the display window focused, press **F11**. The browser chrome — address bar,
tabs, bookmarks — disappears and the display fills the monitor.

Press **F11** again at any time to come back out.

:::warning[Keep the scan tab and the display tab both open]
The display is driven by the scan page **in the same browser**. If you close the
scan tab, or open the display in a different browser or on a different computer,
it will sit there showing the idle screen and never update.
:::

## Step 4 — Go back to the scan tab and work

Click back onto your operator screen. The **Boarding Pass Number** field needs
focus for the hardware scanner to work, so click into it before the first
passenger.

You do **not** need to look at the second monitor while you work — the **Passenger
Display Preview** panel on your own screen shows exactly the same thing, live.

## What passengers see

### Idle — between scans

A dark screen reading **Ready For Pre-Immigration** / _"Waiting for next scanned
passenger..."_ This is also what it shows when the display has just been opened.

![The Passenger Display in its ready state](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/01-display.png)
_The idle display — the public-facing monitor before the first scan._

### Success — the passenger may proceed

A **green** screen with a large tick, the word **Success**, the **passenger's
name**, and their operator and departure time.

![The Passenger Display showing a successful pre-immigration scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/02-success.png)
_A successful scan — the passenger is cleared to Pre-Immigration._

### Rejected — the passenger must stop

A **red** screen with a cross, the word **Rejected.**, and a short message.

![The Passenger Display showing a rejected scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/03-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the passenger is not cleared._

:::info[The display never shows internal detail]
Passengers see only two kinds of real reason — **gate window** messages
(_"Pre-immigration gate is not open yet…"_) and **"Invalid boarding pass number."**

Every other rejection — wrong status, wrong port, a configuration problem — appears
on the public screen as **"Please Check with our terminal staff"**. The real reason
is shown on **your** screen only. Nobody's travel status is put on a large public
monitor.
:::

### Back to idle

About **10 seconds** after each scan the result fades and the display returns to
**Ready For Pre-Immigration**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The display stays on **Ready For Pre-Immigration** while you scan | The scan tab was closed, or the display was opened from a different browser / machine | Re-open the display from the **Open Passenger Display** button on the scan page, and leave both tabs open |
| The display is on the wrong monitor | It opened on the operator screen | Drag the window across, then press **F11** |
| Browser tabs and the address bar are visible to passengers | Not in full-screen | Click the display window, press **F11** |
| It shows an old passenger's result | Normal — results stay up for about 10 seconds | Wait, or scan the next passenger |
