Pre-immigration is the **first scan point**. A passenger arrives holding a boarding
pass; you scan it, check their passport against what the screen shows, and let them
through to immigration.

> **Pre-Immigration Scan → scan the boarding pass → Passport Check card → wave them through**

What the scan does: it moves the passenger from **CheckedIn** to
**PreImmigration**, and drops the trip's Check-In count by one while raising its
Pre-Imm count by one.

:::warning[Restricted access]
This page is only available to staff who hold the pre-immigration scan permission.
:::

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context). **This is not
  optional** — the **Scan Pre-Immigration** button stays disabled until a port is
  selected, and the page shows an amber banner: _"No port selected. Choose a port
  context in Settings before scanning."_ with a **Go to Settings** shortcut.
- The passenger is **CheckedIn** on a trip that is **Scheduled** (and inside its
  gate window) or **Boarding**.
- The passenger display is set up on the second screen — see
  [Passenger Display Setup](/terminal-operator/pre-immigration-display).

## Step 1 — Open Pre-Immigration Scan

In the sidebar, open **Operational → Pre-Immigration Scan**
(route: `/pre-immigration-scan`). The header shows your **Active Port** — for
example, _Active Port: WFC - Waterfront Terminal_. Check it matches where you are
standing.

The page is in two halves: the **scan controls** on the left, and the **Passenger
Display Preview** on the right, which mirrors exactly what the passenger-facing
screen is showing.

![The Pre-Immigration Scan page before the first scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/01-display.png)
_The Pre-Immigration Scan page, ready for the first passenger — scan controls on the
left, Passenger Display Preview on the right._

## Step 2 — Scan the boarding pass

Click into the **Boarding Pass Number** field. Then either:

- **scan the barcode** with the hardware scanner — the number types itself and
  submits, or
- **type the number and press Enter**, or type it and click
  **Scan Pre-Immigration**.

A boarding pass number is always **16 characters**. The field is cleared
automatically after every scan, so you can go straight into the next passenger.

## Step 3 — Read the result

### Accepted

A green **Passport Check #_[BP number]_** card appears with the passenger's
document details — **No**, **Name**, **Gender**, **Date Of Birth**,
**Nationality**, **Issuance Country**, **Issue Date** and **Expiry Date** — and
below it a second green card with the **trip**: code, operator, route, gate window
and departure time.

The card also shows **Previous Status**: the passenger's status **before** this
scan. On a normal first scan it reads **CheckedIn**. If it reads
**PreImmigration**, the card turns red instead — see
[When a pass has already been used](#when-a-pass-has-already-been-used).

Compare the passport in your hand against the card. If it matches, the passenger
goes through.

At the same moment the passenger display turns **green** and shows **Success** with
the passenger's name, their operator and the departure time.

![The Pre-Immigration Scan page after a successful scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/02-success.jpg)
_An accepted scan — the Passport Check card with **Previous Status: CheckedIn** and
the trip card on the left, the green **Success** preview on the right._

### Rejected

A red **Invalid BP** box appears on your screen with the reason. The passenger
display turns **red** and shows **Rejected.**

![The Pre-Immigration Scan page after a rejected scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/03-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the red **Invalid BP** box on the left, the red **Rejected.**
preview on the right._

:::info[The passenger never sees the real reason]
Only two kinds of message are passed through to the public display: **gate window**
messages and **"Invalid boarding pass number."** Everything else — wrong status,
wrong port, configuration problems — collapses to the generic
**"Please Check with our terminal staff"**.

This is deliberate. The passenger standing at the desk should not be told, on a
large screen, that they are already recorded as Boarded. **You** see the real
reason on the operator screen; they see a polite instruction to talk to you.
:::

## Step 4 — Repeat

The field is already cleared and ready. Keep scanning. The result card stays on
screen until the next scan replaces it, and the passenger display returns to its
idle **"Ready For Pre-Immigration"** state about **10 seconds** after each scan.

## Rejection messages and what to do

| What you see on the operator screen | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Please enter boarding pass number."** | You clicked Scan with an empty field. | Scan or type the number. |
| **"Invalid boarding pass number."** | The number is not exactly 16 characters, or no passenger anywhere has it. | Re-scan. If the barcode is damaged, look the passenger up by name on the trip and read the BP number off their row. |
| **"Passenger boarding pass is not valid for the currently selected port."** | The pass belongs to a trip departing from a **different port**. | Check your **Active Port** in Settings. If the port is right, the passenger is at the wrong terminal. |
| **"Passenger must be CheckedIn to move to PreImmigration. Current status: Boarded."** (or PreImmigration, or Cancelled) | The passenger is not at the stage this scan expects. **Boarded** usually means they already went through; **Cancelled** means their booking was cancelled. | For Boarded, see [Revert Boarding](/terminal-operator/revert-boarding). For Cancelled, send them to the counter. |
| **"Trip must be in Scheduled or Boarding status to scan at pre-immigration gate. Current status: Closed."** (or Departed, or Cancelled) | The trip has moved past the point where pre-immigration makes sense. | They have missed the sailing. Send them to the counter. |
| **"Pre-immigration gate is not open yet. Gate opens at HH:mm (PORT time)."** | The trip is still **Scheduled** and you are scanning **before** gate open. | Wait, or widen the window with [Update Timings](/terminal-operator/update-timings). |
| **"Pre-immigration gate is already closed. Gate closed at HH:mm (PORT time)."** | The trip is still **Scheduled** and you are scanning **after** gate close. | Extend **Gate Close** with [Update Timings](/terminal-operator/update-timings), or set the trip to [Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding) — the window stops applying once it is. |
| **"Port context is required. Please select an active port in terminal portal."** | No active port is set. | [Set your port context](/terminal-staff/set-port-context). |

:::tip[The gate window only applies while the trip is Scheduled]
Once the trip is **Boarding**, gate open and gate close no longer block a
pre-immigration scan — from that point the rule is status alone. This matches the
boarding scan, which never checks gate times.
:::

## When a pass has already been used

If you scan a passenger who is **already PreImmigration**, the scan is not
rejected and nothing changes: their status stays **PreImmigration** and they are
not counted twice. But the **Passport Check** card turns **red** and warns you:

> **Boarding pass already used** — Already cleared pre-immigration at _HH:mm_.
> Check their passport against the details below.

The time is when this pass **first** cleared pre-immigration, in the port's time,
and **Previous Status** reads **PreImmigration**. The passenger display still shows
the green **Success** — the warning is on your screen only.

![The Pre-Immigration Scan page after scanning a pass that was already used](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/09-rescan-success.jpg)
_A repeat scan — the red **Boarding pass already used** warning and **Previous
Status: PreImmigration** on the left, while the passenger display still shows
**Success**._

| Previous Status | What it means | Card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CheckedIn** | A normal first scan. The passenger has just been moved to PreImmigration. | Green |
| **PreImmigration** | This pass had already cleared pre-immigration before this scan. | Red — **Boarding pass already used** |

What to do with a red card:

- **The passport matches the card** — usually the same passenger scanned twice:
  you were not sure the first scan registered, or they stepped back and came
  through again. Let them through.
- **The passport does not match the card** — someone may be using a **copy** of
  another passenger's boarding pass. Do not let them through, and raise it with
  your supervisor.

## Made a mistake?

If you scanned someone who should not have gone through, use
[Revert Pre-Immigration](/terminal-operator/revert-pre-immigration) — the **Revert
To Check-In** button is right there on the result card.
