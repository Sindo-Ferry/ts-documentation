Board the wrong person, or board someone who then has to come back off the vessel?
**Revert To Pre-Immigration** undoes the boarding scan and returns them to
**PreImmigration**, freeing the count.

> **Boarding Scan → scan the pass → Revert To Pre-Immigration → Confirm Revert**

:::warning[Restricted access]
Reverting needs the **revert boarding** permission, which is separate from the scan
permission. If you can scan but see no **Revert To Pre-Immigration** button, that is
why.
:::

## When you can revert

| Condition | Required |
| --- | --- |
| The passenger's status | **Boarded** |
| The trip's status | **Boarding** |

The trip condition is the one that catches people out: **once the trip is Closed,
you can no longer revert a boarding**. Reverting is a gate-side correction, and the
gate is shut.

## Step 1 — Bring the passenger up on screen

The **Revert To Pre-Immigration** button lives on the **green result card**.

- Just scanned them? The card is already there — go to Step 2.
- Card gone? **Scan their boarding pass again.** Re-scanning someone already
  Boarded is harmless — it changes nothing and returns their details — and brings
  the card back with the button on it.

![The boarding result card with the Revert To Pre-Immigration button](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/06-revert.png)
_The green result card — **Revert To Pre-Immigration** sits at its bottom._

## Step 2 — Click Revert To Pre-Immigration

The red button sits at the bottom of the result card.

## Step 3 — Confirm

> **Revert Boarding** — This will move **_[passenger name]_** back to
> Pre-Immigration status. They can be re-scanned for boarding when ready.

![The Revert Boarding confirmation dialog](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/07-confirm-revert.png)
_The confirmation names the passenger being reverted._

Click **Confirm Revert** (or **Cancel** to back out).

## Outcome

- The passenger's status returns to **PreImmigration**.
- The trip's **Boarded** count drops by one and its **Pre-Imm** count goes back up,
  in the Boarding Trips panel.
- The result card clears.
- They can be **scanned aboard again** whenever they are ready — same pass, same
  number.

## If the revert is refused

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _"Trip must be in Boarding status to revert boarding. Current status: Closed."_ | The trip has been closed. | Too late here. If the record genuinely needs correcting, use [Update a Passenger's Status](/terminal-operator/update-passenger-status) from the trip's passenger table. |
| _"Passenger must be Boarded to revert boarding. Current status: PreImmigration."_ | Already reverted. | Nothing to do. |

## Correcting a wrong boarding, step by step

The classic case: two passengers hand over passes together, you scan the wrong one.

1. On **Boarding Scan**, scan the pass you scanned by mistake → the green card
   appears (re-scanning is harmless).
2. Click **Revert To Pre-Immigration** → **Confirm Revert**. They are back to
   **PreImmigration**.
3. Scan the **correct** passenger's pass. They board normally.
4. If the first passenger also needs to go all the way back to **CheckedIn** — say
   they are leaving the terminal — switch to **Pre-Immigration Scan**, scan the same
   pass, and use
   [Revert To Check-In](/terminal-operator/revert-pre-immigration).

:::tip[Do this before the trip closes]
Both reverts stop working once the trip is **Closed**. If you notice a mistake as
the vessel is about to sail, fix it **before** you
[set the trip as Close](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-close) — afterwards the
correction is a records job rather than a gate job.
:::
