The boarding gate is the last scan point. A passenger presents their boarding pass,
you scan it, and the display tells them which **berth** to walk to.

> **Boarding Scan → scan the boarding pass → passenger is Boarded → "Proceed to Berth #…"**

What the scan does: it moves the passenger to **Boarded** and raises that trip's
**Boarded** count.

:::warning[Restricted access]
This page is only available to staff who hold the boarding scan permission.
:::

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context). The **Scan**
  button stays disabled until you do, with the hint _"Choose the port context from
  Settings before scanning."_
- The trip has been [set to Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding) with
  a gate and a berth assigned.
- The passenger display is running on the second screen — see
  [Passenger Display Setup](/terminal-operator/boarding-display).

## Step 1 — Open Boarding Scan

In the sidebar, open **Operational → Boarding Scan**
(route: `/current-departure-trips/scan-boarding`). The header shows your **Active
Port**.

![The Boarding Scan page — scan field on the left, Boarding Trips on the right](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/04-operator-display-ready.png)
_The Boarding Scan page — the scan field on the left, and every boarding trip at your port on the right._

:::info[One page covers every boarding trip]
You do **not** pick a trip before scanning. There is a single scan field, and the
system works out which trip each boarding pass belongs to and updates that trip's
count. When three vessels are boarding at once, one officer at one screen handles
all three.
:::

## Step 2 — Check the Boarding Trips panel

The right-hand panel lists every trip at your active port that is currently
**Boarding** or **Closed**. Each card shows:

- The trip **code** and its **status** badge (**Boarding** / **Closed**).
- **Operator**, **Vessel**, **Departure Time**, **Gate / Berth** and **Destination
  Port**.
- Live counts — **Check In**, **Pre-Imm** and **Boarded**.
- **View PreImm Passengers** (on a Boarding trip) — opens a searchable table of the
  passengers who have cleared pre-immigration and are waiting to board.
- **Set As Close** (on a **Boarding** trip) or **Set As Departed** (on a **Closed**
  trip).
- A **Last Call Recommended** banner when departure is close and passengers are
  still waiting — see [Last Call](/terminal-operator/last-call).

The panel **refreshes itself every 10 seconds** while you are idle, and refreshes
immediately after every scan. There is a manual **refresh** button too.

If it reads _"No boarding or closed trips for now."_, no trip at your port has been
opened for boarding yet. Go and
[set the trip to Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding) first.

## Step 3 — Scan

Click into the **Boarding Pass Number** field, then either **scan the barcode** or
**type the 16-character number and press Enter**. You can also click **Scan**.

After every scan the field clears itself and focus returns to it automatically, so
you can work continuously without touching the mouse.

## Step 4 — Read the result

### Accepted

A **green card** appears with the passenger's document details — **No**, **Name**,
**Gender**, **Date Of Birth**, **Nationality**, **Issuance Country**, **Issue
Date**, **Expiry Date** — and the matching trip's **Boarded** count goes up in the
Boarding Trips panel.

The card also shows **Previous Status**: the passenger's status **before** this
scan. Normally it reads **PreImmigration**, or **CheckedIn** if they skipped
pre-immigration. If it reads **Boarded**, the card turns red instead — see
[When a pass has already been used](#when-a-pass-has-already-been-used).

The passenger display turns green: **Success — Proceed to Berth #_[berth]_**, with
the operator's logo and the destination port.

![The operator screen after a successful boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/05-scan-result.jpg)
_A successful scan — passenger details with **Previous Status**, a **Revert To Pre-Immigration** button, and the trip's Boarded count updated._

![The Passenger Display showing a successful boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/02-passenger-display-success.png)
_The passenger-facing display — the berth number is the thing the passenger needs._

### Rejected

A red **Invalid BP** box appears on your screen with the reason, and the display
turns red.

![The Passenger Display showing a rejected boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/03-passenger-display-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the passenger is not boarded._

## A passenger may board straight from Checked-In

Skipping pre-immigration is **allowed**. A passenger whose status is **CheckedIn**
can be scanned at the gate and goes straight to **Boarded** — no error, no warning.
The card shows **Previous Status: CheckedIn**, and the trip's **Check In** count
drops instead of its **Pre-Imm** count.

This is intentional: some terminals do not operate a pre-immigration desk on every
sailing, and a passenger who slipped past it should not be stopped at the gate.

## Rejection messages and what to do

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Please enter boarding pass number."** | You clicked Scan with an empty field. | Scan or type the number. |
| **"Invalid boarding pass number."** | Not 16 characters, or no passenger has that number. | Re-scan. If the barcode is damaged, find the passenger in **View PreImm Passengers** and read their BP number. |
| **"Passenger boarding pass is not valid for the currently selected port."** | The pass belongs to a trip from a **different origin port**. | Check your **Active Port**. This check runs **before** the passenger's status is looked at, so you will see it even for a passenger who is already Boarded. |
| **"Trip must be in Boarding status to mark passenger as Boarded. Current status: Scheduled."** | The trip has not been opened for boarding yet. | [Set it to Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding). |
| **"Trip must be in Boarding status to mark passenger as Boarded. Current status: Closed."** (or Departed) | Boarding for that trip is over. | They have missed it. Send them to the counter. |
| **"Passenger must be CheckedIn or PreImmigration to move to Boarding. Current status: Cancelled."** | The booking was cancelled. | Send them to the counter. |
| **"Port context is required. Please select an active port in terminal portal."** | No active port is set. | [Set your port context](/terminal-staff/set-port-context). |

:::info[What the passenger sees when a scan fails]
Only **"Invalid boarding pass number."** is passed through to the public display.
Everything else shows as **"Please Check with our terminal staff"**. The real reason
stays on your screen.
:::

## When a pass has already been used

If you scan a passenger who is **already Boarded**, the scan is not rejected and
nothing changes: their status stays **Boarded** and the trip's **Boarded** count
does not move. But the result card turns **red** and warns you:

> **Boarding pass already used** — Already boarded at _HH:mm_. Check their passport
> against the details below.

The time is when this pass **first** boarded, in the port's time, and **Previous
Status** reads **Boarded**. The passenger display still shows the green **Success**
— the warning is on your screen only.

![The Boarding Scan page after scanning a pass that was already used](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/09-rescan-success.jpg)
_A repeat scan — the red **Boarding pass already used** warning and **Previous Status: Boarded**._

| Previous Status | What it means | Card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PreImmigration** | A normal first scan. The passenger has just boarded. | Green |
| **CheckedIn** | A first scan of a passenger who skipped pre-immigration. They have just boarded. | Green |
| **Boarded** | This pass had already boarded before this scan. | Red — **Boarding pass already used** |

What to do with a red card:

- **The passport matches the card** — usually the same passenger scanned twice:
  you were not sure the first scan registered, or they stepped back and came
  through again. Let them through.
- **The passport does not match the card** — someone may be using a **copy** of
  another passenger's boarding pass. Do not let them board, and raise it with your
  supervisor.

## Made a mistake?

Use [Revert Boarding](/terminal-operator/revert-boarding) — the **Revert To
Pre-Immigration** button is on the result card.
