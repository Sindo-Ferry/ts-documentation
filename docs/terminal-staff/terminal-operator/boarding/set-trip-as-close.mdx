Closing a trip ends boarding. After it, no more passengers can be scanned aboard,
and the trip is ready to be recorded as departed.

> **Boarding Scan (or Current Trips) → Set As Close → confirm**

:::warning[This cannot be undone]
Closing is **final**. Boarding cannot be reopened, and the
[boarding revert](/terminal-operator/revert-boarding) stops working. Make your
corrections **before** you close.
:::

## Before you close

- The trip is in **Boarding** status. **Set As Close** is not offered on a
  **Scheduled** trip — it has to be opened for boarding first.
- Everyone who is going is aboard. Check the trip card's **Pre-Imm** count: anything
  above zero is a passenger inside the terminal who has not boarded.
- Any [last call](/terminal-operator/last-call) has been made.
- Any wrong scans have been
  [reverted](/terminal-operator/revert-boarding).

## Where you can close a trip

Three places, same dialog:

1. **From the Boarding Scan page** — click **Set As Close** at the top of that
   trip's card in the Boarding Trips panel. This is the usual one, because it is
   where the gate officer already is.
2. **From the trip's detail page** — open **Operational → Current Trips**, click the
   trip, then **Set As Close** at the top right.
3. **From the Current Trips list** — the row's **⋯** menu offers **Set As Close**
   directly.

## Confirm

A dialog opens headed **Set As Close**, with the trip's **code · date · departure
time** beneath it, and the text:

> Close boarding for this trip. No further passengers can board afterwards, and the
> trip can then be departed. This moves the trip forward and cannot be undone.

![The Set As Close confirmation dialog](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/08-confirm-close.png)
_The Set As Close confirmation, opened from the Boarding Scan page._

Click **Set As Close** to commit, or **Cancel** to back out.

### If passengers are still in Pre-Immigration

An amber warning appears above that text:

> ⚠ **_N_ passenger(s) still in Pre-Immigration**
> They have not boarded yet. Closing this trip will prevent them from boarding.

![The Set As Close dialog warning that a passenger is still in Pre-Immigration](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/07-close-preimm-warning.png)
_Closing a trip that still has a passenger in Pre-Immigration — the amber warning appears above the usual confirmation text._

The warning does **not** block you. It is there so the closure is a deliberate
decision rather than an accident. If those passengers are still expected:

1. Click **Cancel**.
2. Make a [last call](/terminal-operator/last-call) and board them from
   [Boarding Scan](/terminal-operator/boarding-scan).
3. Close the trip afterwards.

Otherwise go ahead and close — they will be left behind.

:::tip[The count in the dialog is fresh]
The pre-immigration number is **re-read when the dialog opens**, not taken from the
page you were looking at. So it reflects scans made seconds ago at another desk,
not a stale figure.
:::

## Outcome

- The trip's status becomes **Closed**.
- **Set As Departed** becomes available, in the same places.
- On the Boarding Scan page the trip **stays visible** in the Boarding Trips panel
  with a **Closed** badge, so you can depart it without going anywhere else.
- Any boarding scan against it is now rejected with _"Trip must be in Boarding
  status to mark passenger as Boarded. Current status: Closed."_
- The **manifest** becomes downloadable from the trip's detail page under
  **Departure Trips** — see
  [Download the Manifest](/terminal-operator/download-manifest).

![The page after closing a trip, with Set As Departed available](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/05-close-outcome.png)
_After closing, the trip is **Closed** and **Set As Departed** is available._

## Status order cannot be skipped

| On a trip that is… | **Set As Close** is… |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled | **not offered** — set it to [Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding) first |
| Boarding | offered |
| Closed / Departed / Cancelled | **not offered** |

Force it through and the system answers _"Only boarding trips can be closed.
Current status is …"_
