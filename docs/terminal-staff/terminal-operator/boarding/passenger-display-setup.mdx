The **Passenger Display** at the boarding gate does one job well: it tells the
passenger, in letters readable from several metres away, **which berth to walk to**.

> **Boarding Scan → Open Passenger Display → drag to the second screen → F11**

## Step 1 — Open the display

On the **Boarding Scan** page, click **Open Passenger Display** at the top right.
It opens in a **new browser tab**.

## Step 2 — Move it to the second screen

Drag the tab out into its own window and move that window onto the monitor facing
the passengers.

## Step 3 — Full-screen it

Press **F11**. Press it again to leave full-screen.

:::warning[Keep both tabs open in the same browser]
The display is driven by the Boarding Scan page **in the same browser on the same
machine**. Close the scan tab, or open the display elsewhere, and it will sit on its
idle screen and never update.
:::

## Step 4 — Back to the scan tab

Click back onto the scan tab. Clicking **Open Passenger Display** already returns
focus to the **Boarding Pass Number** field for you, and focus returns there
automatically after each scan — so once you start, you can work without touching
the mouse.

## What passengers see

### Idle — between scans

A dark screen reading **Ready For Boarding** / _"Waiting for next scanned
passenger..."_

![The Passenger Display in its ready-for-scan state at the boarding gate](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/01-passenger-display-ready.png)
_The idle display at the boarding gate._

### Success — proceed to the berth

A **green** screen with a tick, the word **Success**, then in the largest type on
the screen: **Proceed to Berth #_[berth code]_**. Below that, the operator's logo
(or the operator's name if no logo is set) and the **destination port**.

![The Passenger Display showing a successful boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/02-passenger-display-success.png)
_A successful scan — the berth number is what the passenger acts on._

:::tip[The berth comes from the trip]
The berth shown is the one assigned when the trip was
[set to Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding). If passengers are being
sent to the wrong berth, that assignment is what to check.
:::

### Rejected — stop and speak to staff

A **red** screen with a cross, the word **Rejected.**, the passenger's name, and a
short message.

![The Passenger Display showing a rejected boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/03-passenger-display-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the passenger is not boarded._

:::info[The display never shows internal detail]
Only **"Invalid boarding pass number."** is passed through to the public screen.
Every other rejection — wrong port, wrong trip status, a cancelled booking —
appears as **"Please Check with our terminal staff"**. The real reason is on your
screen only.
:::

### Back to idle

About **10 seconds** after each scan the result clears and the display returns to
**Ready For Boarding**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display stays on **Ready For Boarding** while you scan | Scan tab closed, or display opened from another browser / machine | Re-open it from **Open Passenger Display** and leave both tabs open |
| Wrong berth shown | Wrong berth assigned to the trip | Check the trip card's **Gate / Berth**; it was set at [Set As Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding) |
| Operator name instead of a logo | No logo configured for that operator | Cosmetic only — ask an administrator to upload one under [Operators](/terminal-staff/operators) |
| Browser chrome visible to passengers | Not in full-screen | Click the display window, press **F11** |
