Setting a trip to **Boarding** is the moment the day's operation starts for that
sailing. It does two things at once: it **opens boarding**, and it **assigns the
gate and the berth** passengers will be sent to.

> **Current Trips → open the trip → Set As Boarding → pick Gate & Berth → confirm**

:::warning[Trip status only moves forward]
**Scheduled → Boarding → Closed → Departed** (or **Cancelled**). Each action is
only available at the right status, and none of them can be undone. **Set As
Boarding** is offered on **Scheduled** trips only.
:::

:::note
You need permission to view Current Trips and assign trips to boarding. The
screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context). **Current
  Trips only shows today's trips at your active port**, so if the list looks empty
  or wrong, check the port first.
- The [gate](/terminal-staff/gates) and [berth](/terminal-staff/berths) you intend
  to use exist at that port and are marked **Active**.

## Step 1 — Open Current Trips and select the trip

In the sidebar, open **Operational → Current Trips**
(route: `/current-departure-trips`). This lists **today's** departure trips with
their vessel, destination, departure time, status and live passenger counts.

Click the trip's row to open its **detail page**.

![Current Departure Trips list](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/01-current-trips.png)
_The Current Departure Trips list — today's trips at your active port._

## Step 2 — Click Set As Boarding

On the trip's detail page, while the **Status** is **Scheduled**, click **Set As
Boarding** at the top right. Until you assign them, the **Gate / Berth** field
reads `- / -`.

![Scheduled trip detail with the Set As Boarding button](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/01-set-as-boarding.png)
_A Scheduled trip. The **Set As Boarding** button is at the top right._

:::tip[You can also do this without opening the trip]
The **⋯** menu on the trip's row in the Current Trips list offers **Set As
Boarding** directly. The dialog is the same.
:::

## Step 3 — Choose a gate and a berth

A dialog opens: **Set As Boarding — Assign a gate and berth for _[trip code]_ to
begin boarding.**

1. Click **Gate** and pick one from the **Select active gate** picker.
2. Click **Berth** and pick one the same way.
3. Click **Set As Boarding** to confirm.

![Assign a gate and berth dialog](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/02-assign-gate-berth.png)
_The Set As Boarding dialog — choose a Gate and Berth, then confirm._

![Select Gate picker](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/03-select-gate.png)
_The gate picker lists active gates for the trip's origin port; the berth picker works the same way._

:::warning[Both are required]
The confirm button stays **disabled until a gate and a berth are both selected**.
You cannot open boarding without them.
:::

:::tip[If the gate or berth you want isn't listed]
The pickers are filtered to gates and berths that are **Active** and **belong to the
trip's origin port**. If yours is missing, check it under
[Gates](/terminal-staff/gates) or [Berths](/terminal-staff/berths) — it is probably
inactive, or registered against a different port.
:::

## Step 4 — Outcome

The trip's **Status** changes to **Boarding**, the gate and berth are saved onto the
trip, and a prompt appears:

> **Trip Set As Boarding** — Open the Boarding Scan page now?

Choose **Open Boarding Scan** to launch the
[Boarding Scan](/terminal-operator/boarding-scan) page in a new tab, or **Not Now**
if someone else at the gate is already running it.

From here the trip is live:

- It appears in the **Boarding Trips** panel on
  [Boarding Scan](/terminal-operator/boarding-scan), where gate staff scan
  passengers aboard.
- Passengers can still clear
  [pre-immigration](/terminal-operator/pre-immigration-scan) — and once the trip is
  Boarding, the **gate open / close window no longer restricts pre-immigration
  scanning**.
- You can still
  [check in NTL / LM passengers](/terminal-operator/check-in-ntl-lm) onto it and
  [update its timings](/terminal-operator/update-timings).
- When boarding is done, [close the trip](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-close).
