An **AdHoc** trip is a one-off sailing — an extra vessel laid on for a surge, a
charter, a replacement service. It is not produced by a
[timeslot](/terminal-operator/create-timeslot), so you add it by hand.

> **Departure Trips → New AdHoc Trip → fill in the form → Create Trip**

:::note
You need permission to manage departure trips. The screenshots below use
`https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- The **operator**, the **route**, and an **active vessel** for that operator exist.

## Step 1 — Open Departure Trips

In the sidebar, open **Management → Departure Trips** (route: `/departure-trips`).

The page opens on the **Calendar** view and has a **Calendar / List** toggle at the
top right. By default both views show the **coming week** — today plus the next six
days — filtered by **Trip Date**, and can be narrowed further with **Show Filters**.

- The **Calendar** (_Departure Trip Calendar_) lays trips out by **departure time**
  (rows) across the **days** of the window (columns). Each card shows the trip code,
  operator, route, status and load.

  ![The Departure Trip Calendar showing the coming week](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/01-calendar.png)
  _The calendar view — one week of trips, each card with a **View** button and a **⋯** menu._

- The **List** shows one row per trip with its **Date**, **Code**, **Operator**,
  **Route**, **Type**, **Departure** (and gate window), **Status**, and passenger
  counts (**PAX**).

  ![The Departure Trips list view](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/02-list.png)
  _The list view — note the **Type** column, which tells Scheduled and AdHoc apart._

## Step 2 — Click New AdHoc Trip

Click **New AdHoc Trip** (top right of the **List** view) to open the **New AdHoc
Departure Trip** form (route: `/departure-trips/new`).

## Step 3 — Fill in the form

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Operator** | The operator running this trip. |
| **Vessel** | Enabled once you pick an operator; lists only that operator's **active** vessels. |
| **Route** | The route the trip sails. |
| **Trip Date** | Today or later. |
| **Code Suffix** _(optional)_ | Needed only if the trip would otherwise share its code with another trip on the same operator, route, date and departure. |
| **Gate Open** / **Gate Close** / **Departure** | Gate Open must be at or before Gate Close, and Departure must be after Gate Close. |

Then click **Create Trip**.

![The New AdHoc Departure Trip form filled in](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/05-adhoc-form.png)
_Creating an AdHoc trip for Waterfront Ferry on the WFC–HFC route, departing 2:30 PM._

## Outcome

The trip is created with status **Scheduled** and appears in both the **calendar**
and the **list** alongside the generated trips, distinguished by its
**Type = AdHoc** badge. From that moment it behaves exactly like any other trip:
passengers can be checked in against it, and it moves through
[Boarding](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-boarding) →
[Closed](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-close) →
[Departed](/terminal-operator/set-trip-as-depart).

![The AdHoc trip in the list with a Type = AdHoc badge](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/06-adhoc-in-list.png)
_The new trip in the list, tagged **AdHoc** (all sync-generated trips are **Scheduled**)._

:::info[Scheduled vs AdHoc — why the Type matters]
Every trip carries a **Type**. **Scheduled** trips come from a
[Trip Sync Job](/terminal-operator/generate-trips) run against your timeslots;
**AdHoc** trips are the one-off trips you create here.

A sync job only ever touches **Scheduled** trips, so your **AdHoc** trips are never
deleted or reconciled away by it. If you need a recurring sailing rather than a
one-off, create a [timeslot](/terminal-operator/create-timeslot) instead — otherwise
you will be adding the same AdHoc trip by hand every day.
:::
