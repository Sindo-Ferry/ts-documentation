Every task in the Terminal Portal starts by signing in. This short guide covers
the login step; other guides link here instead of repeating it.

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- A registered account with a **confirmed email**, **activated** by an
  administrator — see [Register an Account](/register-account).

## Step 1 — Open the login page and sign in

Open the portal and go to the login page. Enter your registered **Email** and
**Password**, then click **Sign In**.

![Terminal Portal sign-in page](/img/terminal-staff/sign-in/01-login.png)
_The Terminal Portal sign-in page._

## Step 2 — Land on the Dashboard

After signing in you land on the **Dashboard**, which summarizes today's trips,
live boarding, and upcoming departures. From the sidebar you can then reach
**Current Trips**, **Trip Synchronizations**, and the other modules.

![Terminal Portal dashboard](/img/terminal-staff/sign-in/02-dashboard.png)
_Dashboard shown after a successful login._

:::tip
If the page returns you to the login screen, your session has expired — simply
sign in again.
:::

## Troubleshooting

- **"Email not confirmed. Please check your inbox."** — confirm your email
  first; use the **Resend confirmation email** button if the link expired. See
  [Register an Account](/register-account#step-2--confirm-your-email).
- **"Login succeeded, but your account is not provisioned for Opusbay access.
  Please contact your administrator."** — your account has not been activated
  yet. Ask your administrator to activate it — see
  [Register an Account](/register-account#step-3--get-activated-by-an-administrator).
