This guide walks terminal staff through generating ferry departure trips in the
**Terminal Portal**:

> **Trip Sync Job → Create New Sync Job → Execute Job → Departure Trips**

A _Trip Sync Job_ reconciles the scheduled departure trips for a date range
against the active [timeslots](/terminal-staff/timeslots). When you execute it, the
portal creates a departure trip for every active timeslot that matches the job's
**operator**, **route**, and **days** on each date in the range (and removes trips
that should no longer exist), so the generated trips become visible in **Departure
Trips**.

:::note
You need an account with permission to manage trip synchronizations. The
screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- Permission to view and manage **Trip Synchronizations** and **Departure Trips**.

## Step 1 — Open Trip Synchronizations

In the sidebar under **Operational**, open **Trip Synchronizations**
(route: `/departure-trip-synchronizations`). This page lists previous sync jobs
with their date range, scope, results (trips created / deleted), and status.

![Trip Synchronizations list](/img/terminal-staff/generate-departure-trips/01-trip-sync-list.png)
_The Trip Synchronizations list — the top job shows **+15 created / Completed** — with the New Sync Job button._

## Step 2 — Create a new sync job

Click **New Sync Job** (top right) and fill in the job configuration:

- **Start Date / End Date** — the period to generate trips for (use the same day
  for a single date).
- **Operator** _(optional)_ — leave blank to include all operators.
- **Route** _(optional)_ — leave as **All Routes** to include every route.
- **Day Filter** _(optional)_ — leave unchecked to include all days, or pick
  specific weekdays / weekends / public holidays.
- **Remarks** _(optional)_ — a note describing the job's purpose.

Then click **Create Sync Job**.

:::note[Date rules]
The **Start Date** cannot be in the past, and the **End Date** must be on or after
the Start Date.
:::

![New Sync Job form](/img/terminal-staff/generate-departure-trips/02-new-sync-job.png)
_The New Sync Job form, scoped to **Waterfront Ferry** over 13–17 Jul 2026._

## Step 3 — Review the preview and execute the job

Creating the job opens its detail page with a **Preview** section showing how
many trips will be created (here, **15**), deleted, and how many conflicts were
detected. Conflicts are skipped during execution.

When the preview looks correct, click **Execute Job** and confirm. The job is
enqueued and processed in the background; its status moves from **Pending** →
**Enqueued** → **Completed**.

![Sync job preview showing 15 trips to create, with the Execute Job button](/img/terminal-staff/generate-departure-trips/03-execute-job.png)
_The preview — **15 to create** — and the Execute Job action._

:::tip[Reading the preview]
The preview compares each date's **active timeslots** against the existing
**Scheduled** trips and buckets the difference into three results:

- **To Create** — an active timeslot has no matching trip yet on that date.
- **To Delete** — a scheduled trip no longer matches any active timeslot **and has
  no check-ins or boarded passengers**, so it is safe to remove.
- **Conflicts** — an orphaned trip **already has passengers / check-ins**; it is
  **skipped** (never deleted) and flagged for manual review.

So **0 to create / 0 to delete / 0 conflicts** means everything is already in
sync — the job is idempotent and safe to re-run.
:::

## Step 4 — Verify the trips in Departure Trips

Open **Departure Trips** (route: `/departure-trips/list`). Filter the **Trip
Date** to the period you just synced to see the generated trips, each with its
code, operator, route, departure time, status, and passenger counts.

:::note[Type = Scheduled]
Every trip generated by a Trip Sync Job has **Type = Scheduled**. One-off trips
added manually are **AdHoc** instead — see [Departure Trips](/terminal-staff/departure-trips).
:::

![Departure Trips list for the synced date](/img/terminal-staff/generate-departure-trips/04-departure-trips.png)
_The generated **Waterfront Ferry** trips — including the **11:20 WFC–TMFT** trips from the new timeslot — for 13–17 Jul._

## What happens during a sync

The job only ever affects **Scheduled** trips — **AdHoc** trips are never touched.
For each date in the range (subject to the **Day Filter**), it compares the **active
timeslots whose operator is also active** against the existing scheduled trips,
matching them by trip code (operator + route + date + departure + suffix):

| Result | When it happens | What execution does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Created** | An active timeslot applies to that date but has **no matching trip** yet. | A new **Scheduled** trip is created from the timeslot (with its vessel and gate times). |
| **Deleted** | A scheduled trip **no longer matches any active timeslot** (orphaned) **and has no check-ins or boarded passengers**. | The orphaned, empty trip is **removed**. |
| **Conflicts** | An orphaned scheduled trip **has check-ins or boarded passengers**. | It is **skipped** (kept) and logged for manual review — reassign or clear the passengers, then re-run. |

:::info[What makes a trip "orphaned"]
A trip becomes orphaned when nothing generates it anymore — for example its timeslot
was **deleted or deactivated**, or its **operator was deactivated**. Orphaned trips
are then **deleted** if empty, or raised as a **conflict** if they already have
passengers. Note that on a **public holiday** date, only timeslots with the **Public
Holiday** flag apply (weekday flags are ignored that day).
:::
