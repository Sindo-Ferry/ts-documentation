**Departure Trips** is where you view and manage all of the terminal's trips — both
the **Scheduled** trips generated by a
[Trip Sync Job](/trip-sync-job) and one-off **AdHoc**
trips added manually. It offers a **calendar** and a **list** view, each with a
filter. By default it shows the **coming week** (today through the next 6 days).

> **Departure Trips → Calendar / List → View · Cancel · Restore · New AdHoc Trip**

:::note
You need permission to view and manage departure trips. The screenshots below use
`https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Calendar and list views

Use the **Calendar / List** toggle (top right) to switch views. Both are filtered
by **Trip Date** (defaulting to the coming week) and can be narrowed further with
**Show Filters**.

- The **Calendar** (*Departure Trip Calendar*) lays trips out by **departure time**
  (rows) across the **days** of the window (columns). Each card shows the trip code,
  operator, route, status, and load.

  ![The Departure Trip Calendar showing the coming week](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/01-calendar.png)
  _The calendar view — one week of trips, each card with a **View** button and a **⋯** menu._

- The **List** shows one row per trip with its **Date**, **Code**, **Operator**,
  **Route**, **Type**, **Departure** (and gate window), **Status**, and passenger
  counts (**PAX**).

  ![The Departure Trips list view](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/02-list.png)
  _The list view — note the **Type** column (Scheduled vs AdHoc)._

## 1. View, cancel, and restore a trip

### Open a trip's detail

In the **calendar**, click a trip **card** or its **View** button to open the
trip's **detail page**. In the **list**, use the row **⋯ Actions → View Detail**.
The detail page shows the trip info, a **status timeline** (Scheduled → Boarding →
Closed → Departed), the passenger list, and a **Download / Cancel** action.

![A departure trip's detail page](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/01-set-as-boarding.png)
_The trip detail page — trip info, status timeline, and passengers._

### Cancel or restore a trip

The row **⋯ Actions** menu (available in **both** the calendar and list) offers
**View Detail** plus, depending on the trip's status, **Cancel Trip** or **Restore
Trip**:

![The Actions menu on a scheduled trip](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/03-actions-menu.png)
_A **Scheduled** trip's Actions menu — **View Detail** and **Cancel Trip**._

- **Cancel Trip** — available for **Scheduled** trips. A trip **cannot be cancelled
  once it has checked-in passengers**. Cancelling sets the trip's status to
  **Cancelled**.
- **Restore Trip** — available for **Cancelled** trips; it returns the trip to
  **Scheduled**. A trip can only be restored while:
  - its **departure time is still in the future**;
  - no other active trip already uses its **code**; and
  - its **operator, route, and vessel are all active**.

:::tip
**Restore** is essentially "un-cancel", so it only appears on **cancelled** trips.
If a restore is blocked, the message tells you why — e.g. reactivate the vessel, or
the departure time has already passed.
:::

## 2. Create an AdHoc trip

An **AdHoc** trip is an **unscheduled, one-off** trip — one that isn't produced by a
timeslot. Authorized users can add one directly here.

1. Click **New AdHoc Trip** (top right) to open the **New AdHoc Departure Trip**
   form (route: `/departure-trips/new`).
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field | Notes |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Operator** | The operator running the trip. |
   | **Vessel** | Enabled after choosing an operator; lists only that operator's **active** vessels. |
   | **Route** | The trip's route. |
   | **Trip Date** | The date of the trip (today or later). |
   | **Code Suffix** _(optional)_ | Distinguishes it from another trip that would otherwise share the same code. |
   | **Gate Open** / **Gate Close** / **Departure** | The trip's timings (Gate Open ≤ Gate Close, and Departure after Gate Close). |

3. Click **Create Trip**.

![The New AdHoc Departure Trip form filled in](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/05-adhoc-form.png)
_Creating an AdHoc trip for Waterfront Ferry on the WFC–HFC route, departing 2:30 PM._

The AdHoc trip then appears in both the **calendar** and **list** views alongside
the scheduled trips — distinguished by its **Type = AdHoc** badge.

![The AdHoc trip in the list with a Type = AdHoc badge](/img/terminal-staff/departure-trips/06-adhoc-in-list.png)
_The new trip in the list, tagged **AdHoc** (all sync-generated trips are **Scheduled**)._

:::info[Scheduled vs AdHoc]
Every trip carries a **Type**: **Scheduled** trips come from a
[Trip Sync Job](/trip-sync-job) run against your
[timeslots](/terminal-staff/timeslots); **AdHoc** trips are the one-off trips you
create here. A sync job only ever touches **Scheduled** trips — your **AdHoc** trips
are never removed by it.
:::
