Some passengers are not part of the normal check-in flow and must be added
manually onto a departure trip:

- **NTL — Not To Land:** a passenger who stays on board and is not landing at the
  destination (e.g. transit or crew-type cases).
- **LM — Last Minute:** a walk-up passenger added right before departure.

This guide covers how terminal staff add either type from a trip's detail page in
the **Terminal Portal**.

:::info[Who checks in whom]
From the Terminal Portal, authorized staff can **only** check in **Not To Land
(NTL)** and **Last Minute (LM)** passengers. Ordinary passengers are checked in
by the ferry operator — see
[Check In a Passenger](/ferry-operator/operator-check-in-passenger). An NTL / LM
passenger can be added as long as the trip has open seats and is not yet
**Closed**, **Departed**, or **Cancelled**.
:::

:::note
You need permission to view **Current Trips** and to check in passengers. The
screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- The departure trip already exists and is visible in **Current Trips**.

## Step 1 — Open Current Trips

In the sidebar under **Operational**, open **Current Trips**
(route: `/current-departure-trips`). This lists today's departure trips with their
vessel, destination, departure time, status, and live passenger counts.

![Current Departure Trips list](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/01-current-trips.png)
_The Current Departure Trips list._

## Step 2 — Select a trip to open its detail page

Click the trip you want to add the passenger to. Its **detail page** opens,
showing Trip Info, Timings, and the **Passengers** section with live counts
(Checked-In, Pre-Immigration, Boarded, Available).

![Trip detail page with the Check In NTL / LM Passenger button](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/02-trip-detail.png)
_The trip detail page. The **Check In NTL / LM Passenger** button is in the Passengers section._

## Step 3 — Click "Check In NTL / LM Passenger"

In the **Passengers** section, click **Check In NTL / LM Passenger**. A dialog
opens, pre-filled with the current **Departure Trip**.

![Check In NTL / LM Passenger dialog with the Passenger Type options](/img/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger/03-check-in-dialog.png)
_The dialog. Use **Passenger Type** to choose between **NotToLand** and **LastMinute**._

## Step 4 — Enter the passenger details and check in

Fill in the passenger's details:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **No** | Passport / ID number. |
| **Name** | Passenger's full name. |
| **Gender** | Optional; defaults to _Unspecified_. |
| **Passenger Type** | **NotToLand** (NTL) or **LastMinute** (LM) — this is the key choice. |
| **Nationality** | Select from the list. |
| **Date of Birth** | Format `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| **Issue Date** _(optional)_ / **Expiry Date** / **Issue Country** | Travel-document details. |

Then click **Check In Passenger**.

:::tip
Pick **NotToLand** for passengers remaining on board (not landing) and
**LastMinute** for walk-ups added just before departure. The type is recorded on
the trip so downstream boarding and manifest reports classify them correctly.
:::

## Outcome

The passenger is added to the trip and appears in the **Passengers** list with:

- **Status: `Checked-In`** — the first stage of the passenger journey. From here
  the passenger progresses through **Pre-Immigration** and **Boarded** as they
  are scanned (or can be **Cancelled**).
- A unique **boarding pass number** — the passenger's reference for the rest of
  the process (the pre-immigration and boarding scans), so be sure it's captured.

The trip's **Checked-In** count and occupied seats increase accordingly.
