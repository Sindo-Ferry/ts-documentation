A departure trip moves through a one-way lifecycle. Terminal staff drive it from
**Current Trips**: open boarding, close boarding once everyone is aboard, then
record the departure.

> **Scheduled → Set As Boarding → Set As Close → Set As Depart → Departed**

:::warning[Restricted access]
**Current Trips** can only be accessed by **authorized operational staff**.
:::

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context).
- The trip exists in **Current Trips**.

## Departure-trip status reference

A departure trip moves through the statuses below. The normal lifecycle is
**Scheduled → Boarding → Closed → Departed**; a trip may instead be **Cancelled**.

| Status | What it means | How it gets there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Planned and listed, but not yet open for boarding. | Created by a [Trip Sync Job](/trip-sync-job) |
| **Boarding** | Boarding is open; passengers can board. | [Set As Boarding](#1-set-as-boarding) (requires gate & berth) |
| **Closed** | Boarding is closed; no more passengers can board; ready for departure. | [Set As Close](#2-set-as-close) |
| **Departed** | The trip has departed; the trip is finalized. | [Set As Depart](#3-set-as-depart) |
| **Cancelled** | The trip has been cancelled and will not run. | Cancel action |

## 1. Set As Boarding

When a scheduled trip is ready for passengers, move it to **Boarding** and assign
the **gate** and **berth** it will use.

> **Current Trips → select the trip → Set As Boarding → assign Gate & Berth**

### Open Current Trips and select the trip

Open **Current Trips** (route: `/current-departure-trips`) and click the trip you
want to board. Its detail page opens.

_(For screenshots of the Current Trips list and the trip detail page, see
[Check In an NTL / LM Passenger](/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger),
Steps 1–2.)_

### Click Set As Boarding

On the trip's detail page, while the **Status** is **Scheduled**, click
**Set As Boarding** (top right). The **Gate / Berth** field shows `- / -` until you
assign them in the next step.

![Scheduled trip detail with the Set As Boarding button](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/01-set-as-boarding.png)
_A Scheduled trip. The **Set As Boarding** button is at the top right._

### Assign a gate and berth

A dialog opens: **Assign a gate and berth … to begin boarding.**

1. Click **Gate** and pick an active gate from the picker (filtered to the trip's
   port and to **Active** gates).
2. Click **Berth** and pick an active berth the same way.
3. Click **Set As Boarding** to confirm.

![Assign a gate and berth dialog](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/02-assign-gate-berth.png)
_The Set As Boarding dialog — choose a Gate and Berth, then confirm._

![Select Gate picker](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/03-select-gate.png)
_The gate picker lists active gates for the trip's port; the berth picker works the same way._

:::tip
Only **active** gates and berths at the trip's port are selectable. If the one you
need isn't listed, check that it exists and is marked active under the port's
master data.
:::

### Boarding begins

Once you confirm, the trip's **Status** changes to **Boarding** and you are taken
to the trip's **Boarding Scan** page, where the boarding process is run.

![The Boarding Scan page shown after a trip is set to Boarding](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/04-outcome.png)
_The Boarding Scan page for a trip that is now Boarding._

From this page you can:

- **See the trip detail** — operator, vessel, departure time, gate / berth,
  destination port, and live counts (Check-In, Pre-Imm, Boarded).
- **Scan boarding passes** — enter or scan a **Boarding Pass Number** to board a
  passenger. See [Boarding Scan](/terminal-staff/boarding-scan) for the full
  scanning workflow.
- **Revert a boarded passenger to Pre-Immigration** — after a successful boarding
  scan, the scanned passenger's card shows a **Revert to Pre-Immigration** button
  that moves that passenger back to the Pre-Immigration state when needed for
  operational reasons. See
  [Boarding Scan → Revert a boarded passenger](/terminal-staff/boarding-scan#step-7--revert-a-boarded-passenger)
  for details.
- **Work the passenger list** — a filterable list that, by default, shows
  passengers who have **cleared pre-immigration and are ready to board** (the
  _Status: PreImmigration_ filter). Search or change the filters as needed.
- **Open the Passenger Display** — launch the separate boarding-pass scan display
  shown to passengers.
- **Close the trip** — when boarding is finished, use **Set As Close** (top right),
  covered next.

You can also [check in NTL / LM passengers](/terminal-staff/check-in-ntl-lm-passenger)
onto the trip while it is boarding.

## 2. Set As Close

When boarding is complete, **close** the trip. Closing stops any further boarding
and marks the trip ready for departure.

:::warning[This cannot be undone]
Closing a trip is **final**. Once closed, **no more passengers can board** and the
trip must be ready for departure — boarding cannot be reopened.
:::

### Where to close a trip

You can set a trip as close from **two** places:

1. **From Current Trips** — open **Current Trips**, select the **Boarding** trip to
   open its detail page, then click **Set As Close**.
2. **From the Boarding Scan page** — when the boarding scan is finished, click
   **Set As Close** at the top right of the Boarding Scan page (shown above).

### Confirm and outcome

A **confirmation** appears — it reminds you that no further passengers can board,
that the trip must be ready for departure, and that the action **cannot be undone**.
Click **Confirm**.

The trip's **Status** changes to **Closed** and a **Set As Departed** action
becomes available. From here you can review the trip and its **boarded passengers**,
then record the departure.

![The page after closing a trip, with Set As Departed available](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/05-close-outcome.png)
_After closing, the trip is **Closed** and **Set As Departed** is available._

## 3. Set As Depart

Setting a trip as **departed** is the final step. It records that the trip has left,
finalizes the trip, and makes its **manifest** available to download or send.

:::warning[Final step — cannot be changed]
Marking a trip as departed **finalizes** it. Once departed, the trip is
**unchangeable** and no further action can be taken on it.
:::

### Steps

1. Open **Current Trips**, select the **Closed** trip to open its detail page, then
   click **Set As Depart**.
2. A **confirmation** appears — click **Confirm**.

### Outcome

The page reloads with the trip now showing the **Departed** status, and a
**Download Manifest** button so you can download (or send) the trip's passenger
manifest — see [Passenger Manifest](/terminal-staff/passenger-manifest).

![The trip detail page after departure — Departed status with Download Manifest](/img/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip/06-departed-outcome.png)
_The finalized, **Departed** trip with its manifest ready to download._

This is the **end of the trip's journey** — the trip is finalized and no further
action can be taken.
