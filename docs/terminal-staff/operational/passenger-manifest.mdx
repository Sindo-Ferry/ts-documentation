Once a trip has been [set as departed](/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip#3-set-as-depart),
its passenger **manifest** becomes available to download from the trip's detail
page. The manifest is the official list of **boarded** passengers on the trip.

> **Current Trips → open the trip → Download Manifest → English / Indonesian**

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## When the manifest is available

The **Download Manifest** button appears on the trip's detail page and is enabled
only once the trip is **Closed** or **Departed**. Before that, the action is
disabled with a hint:

- _"Select a trip to download its manifest."_ — no trip is loaded.
- _"Available once the trip is closed or departed."_ — the trip is still Scheduled
  or Boarding.

:::info[What the manifest contains]
The manifest lists only **Boarded** passengers, ordered by check-in time. For each
passenger it includes the trip details (code, date, departure, route, vessel) and
the passenger's **No (passport / ID)**, **name**, **gender**, **date of birth**,
**nationality**, **issue country**, **issue / expiry dates**, **boarding pass
number**, **passenger type**, and **status**.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have permission to export passenger manifests.
- The trip is **Closed** or **Departed**.

## Step 1 — Open the trip detail page

Open **Current Trips** (route: `/current-departure-trips`) and select the
**Departed** (or Closed) trip. Its detail page shows the trip info, timings, and
the passenger list, with **Download Manifest** at the top right.

![The departed trip detail page with the Download Manifest button](/img/terminal-staff/passenger-manifest/01-download-manifest.png)
_A Departed trip. **Download Manifest** is enabled at the top right._

## Step 2 — Choose a language and download

Click **Download Manifest** and choose the manifest language:

- **English**
- **Indonesian**

![The Download Manifest menu showing English and Indonesian options](/img/terminal-staff/passenger-manifest/02-language-options.png)
_Pick the language for the manifest — the report is generated in that language._

The manifest is generated as an **Excel (`.xlsx`)** file and downloaded to your
browser, named like `passenger-manifest-<TRIP-CODE>-<TRIP-DATE>.xlsx` (for example,
`passenger-manifest-SSFHW202607031300-2026-07-03.xlsx`).

:::tip
You can download the manifest again at any time while the trip stays Closed or
Departed, in either language — the list always reflects the trip's boarded
passengers.
:::
