Once passengers clear pre-immigration, they move to the final **boarding gate**. On
this page, operational staff scan each passenger's boarding pass to board them onto
their trip, with a public **Passenger Display** for the gate.

> **Boarding Scan → scan a boarding pass → the passenger is boarded onto their trip**

:::warning[Restricted access]
This page can only be accessed by **authorized operational staff**.
:::

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context) — the page is
  **scoped to that port**, and scanning is disabled until a port is selected.
- The trip is set to **Boarding** and passengers have cleared
  [pre-immigration](/terminal-staff/pre-immigration-scan).

## Step 1 — Open Boarding Scan

In the sidebar, open **Operational → Boarding Scan** (route:
`/current-departure-trips/scan-boarding`). The page header shows your **Active
Port** — for example, _Active Port: WFC - Waterfront Terminal_.

![The Boarding Scan page — scan field on the left, Boarding Trips on the right](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/04-operator-display-ready.png)
_The Boarding Scan page — the scan field on the left, and every boarding trip at your port on the right._

:::info[Boarding Scan now has its own sidebar entry]
Previously you could only reach this page from a **targeted trip** in
[Current Trips](/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip). It is now a **page of its
own**, opened straight from the sidebar and covering **all** of your port's
boarding trips at once — so gate staff no longer have to re-enter it per trip.
:::

## Step 2 — Check the Boarding Trips list

The right-hand **Boarding Trips** panel lists every trip at your active port that is
currently **Boarding** or **Closed**. Each card shows the trip's details, so you can
confirm what is boarding without leaving the page:

- The trip **code** and its **status** badge (**Boarding** / **Closed**).
- **Operator**, **Vessel**, **Departure Time**, **Gate / Berth**, and
  **Destination Port**.
- Live counts — **Check In**, **Pre-Imm**, and **Boarded**.
- **View PreImm Passengers** — opens the list of passengers who have cleared
  pre-immigration and are ready to board.
- **Set As Close** (on a **Boarding** trip) or **Set As Departed** (on a **Closed**
  trip) — see
  [Manage Current Trip Status](/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip).

Use the **refresh** button (top right of the panel) to pull the latest counts.

:::tip[One scan field serves every trip in the list]
You do **not** select a trip before scanning. The scan is matched on the **boarding
pass number** alone, so it applies across **all** the trips shown — the system
resolves each pass to its own trip and updates that trip's **Boarded** count.
:::

If the panel reads _"No boarding or closed trips for now."_, no trip at your port has
been set to Boarding yet. Set the trip's status to **Boarding** by assigning a
**gate** and **berth** first — see
[Manage Current Trip Status → Set As Boarding](/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip#1-set-as-boarding).

## Step 3 — Set up the Passenger Display

Next to the scanning field, click **Open Passenger Display** to launch the
dedicated **gate interface**. As before, **drag this window onto a second
monitor** and press **F11** for full-screen mode so it faces the passengers.

![The Passenger Display in its ready-for-scan state at the boarding gate](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/01-passenger-display-ready.png)
_The Passenger Display — the ready-for-scan gate screen shown to passengers._

## Step 4 — Scan the boarding pass

Back on your operator screen, click into the **Boarding Pass Number** field. You can
either **type a valid Boarding Pass Number and press Enter**, or use a **hardware
scanner** to scan the physical barcode. Then click **Scan**.

On a valid pass, the operator screen shows a **green result card** with the
passenger's document details — **No**, **Name**, **Gender**, **Date Of Birth**,
**Nationality**, **Issuance Country**, **Issue Date**, and **Expiry Date** — and the
matching trip's **Boarded** count goes up in the Boarding Trips list.

![The operator screen after a successful boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/05-scan-result.png)
_A successful scan on the operator screen — passenger details, a **Revert To Pre-Immigration** button, and the trip's Boarded count updated._

If the pass is not valid, an **Invalid BP** message explains why instead.

## Step 5 — Outcome (operator screen and Passenger Display)

On a successful scan, the operator view instantly processes the boarding pass and
updates the **trip metrics** (Boarded count). At the same time, the **Passenger
Display** on the second screen shows a **Success** or **Reject** message with the
relevant passenger details.

![The Passenger Display showing a successful boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/02-passenger-display-success.png)
_A successful scan — the passenger is boarded._

![The Passenger Display showing a rejected boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/03-passenger-display-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the passenger is not boarded; check the message and try again._

## Step 6 — Revert a boarded passenger

If a boarded passenger needs to be reverted, simply **re-scan their boarding pass
number**. The **Revert To Pre-Immigration** button is shown again for that scanned
boarding pass, and the boarding-gate staff can click it to move the passenger back —
removing them from the active **Boarded** count. Confirm the **Revert Boarding**
dialog with **Confirm Revert**.

:::tip
Reverting returns the passenger to **Pre-Immigration**, so they can be boarded
again with another scan when they are ready.
:::
