Once passengers clear pre-immigration, they move to the final **boarding gate**. On
this page, operational staff scan each passenger's boarding pass to board them onto
the trip, with a public **Passenger Display** for the gate.

> **Current Trips → select the trip → Set As Boarding (gate & berth) → scan boarding pass**

:::warning[Restricted access]
This page can only be accessed by **authorized operational staff**.
:::

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context).
- The trip exists in **Current Trips** and passengers have cleared
  [pre-immigration](/terminal-staff/pre-immigration-scan).

## Step 1 — Open Current Trips and select today's trip

Open **Current Trips** (route: `/current-departure-trips`) and select an active
trip scheduled for today.

## Step 2 — Set the trip to Boarding

Open the boarding module for that trip. If the trip is still **Scheduled**, set its
status to **Boarding** first by assigning a **gate** and **berth** — see
[Manage Current Trip Status → Set As Boarding](/terminal-staff/manage-departure-trip#1-set-as-boarding)
for the full gate/berth steps.

## Step 3 — The Boarding Scan page

Setting the trip to Boarding launches the **Boarding Scan** page for that trip. The
**pre-immigration passenger list** (passengers ready to board) is shown on the
right side.

![The Boarding Scan page for a trip that is now Boarding](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/04-operator-display-ready.png)
_The Boarding Scan page — scan field on the left, ready-to-board passengers on the right._

## Step 4 — Set up the Passenger Display

Next to the scanning field, click **Open Passenger Display** to launch the
dedicated **gate interface**. As before, **drag this window onto a second
monitor** and press **F11** for full-screen mode so it faces the passengers.

![The Passenger Display in its ready-for-scan state at the boarding gate](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/01-passenger-display-ready.png)
_The Passenger Display — the ready-for-scan gate screen shown to passengers._

## Step 5 — Scan the boarding pass

Back on your operator screen, click into the active scanner input field. You can
either **type a valid Boarding Pass Number and press Enter**, or use a **hardware
scanner** to scan the physical barcode.

## Step 6 — Outcome (operator screen and Passenger Display)

On a successful scan, the operator view instantly processes the boarding pass and
updates the **trip metrics** (Boarded count). At the same time, the **Passenger
Display** on the second screen shows a **Success** or **Reject** message with the
relevant passenger details.

![The Passenger Display showing a successful boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/02-passenger-display-success.png)
_A successful scan — the passenger is boarded._

![The Passenger Display showing a rejected boarding scan](/img/terminal-staff/boarding-scan/03-passenger-display-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the passenger is not boarded; check the message and try again._

## Step 7 — Revert a boarded passenger

If a boarded passenger needs to be reverted, simply **re-scan their boarding pass
number**. The **Revert To Pre-Immigration** button is shown again for that scanned
boarding pass, and the boarding-gate staff can click **Revert** to move the
passenger back — removing them from the active **Boarded** count.

:::tip
Reverting returns the passenger to **Pre-Immigration**, so they can be boarded
again with another scan when they are ready.
:::
