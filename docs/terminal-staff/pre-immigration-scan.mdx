Before passengers are allowed into the boarding gate, they must clear the
**pre-immigration** stage. On this page, operational staff scan each passenger's
boarding pass to move them from **Checked-In** to **Pre-Immigration**.

> **Pre-Immigration Scan → Open Passenger Display → scan boarding pass → (revert if needed)**

:::warning[Restricted access]
This page can only be accessed by **authorized operational staff**.
:::

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Terminal Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in).
- You have [set your active port](/terminal-staff/set-port-context).
- Passengers have already been **checked in** for the trip.

## Step 1 — Open the Pre-Immigration Scan view

From the sidebar, open **Pre-Immigration Scan** (route: `/pre-immigration-scan`).

## Step 2 — Set up the Passenger Display

Next to the scanning field, click **Open Passenger Display** to launch the
dedicated **public-facing monitor** interface. For the best setup, **drag this new
window onto a second screen** and press **F11** to enter full-screen mode, so it
faces the passengers while you scan from the operator screen.

![The Passenger Display shown to passengers on a second screen](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/01-display.png)
_The Passenger Display — the public-facing monitor for passengers._

## Step 3 — Scan the boarding pass

Click into the active scanner input field on your operator screen, then **scan**
(or manually type) the passenger's **boarding pass number** and confirm.

## Step 4 — Outcome (operator screen and Passenger Display)

On a successful scan, the system **instantly moves the passenger from Checked-In to
Pre-Immigration**. At the same time, the **Passenger Display** on the second screen
shows a **Success** or **Reject** message along with the relevant passenger details.

![The Passenger Display showing a successful pre-immigration scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/02-success.png)
_A successful scan — the passenger is cleared to Pre-Immigration._

![The Passenger Display showing a rejected scan](/img/terminal-staff/pre-imm-scan/03-error.png)
_A rejected scan — the passenger is not cleared; check the message and try again._

## Step 5 — Revert a scan

If a passenger is scanned by mistake or needs to step back out, use the **revert**
action (**Revert To Check-In**) to instantly return the passenger back to
**Checked-In** status.

:::tip
Reverting is optional and only affects the passenger you scanned. Use it to undo an
accidental scan; the passenger can then be scanned again when they are ready.
:::
