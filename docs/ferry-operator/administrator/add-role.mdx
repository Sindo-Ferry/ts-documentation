A **role** is a named bundle of permissions — _Boarding Gate Officer_,
_Check-in Counter_, _Head of Operations_. You create the role here, give it
permissions, then assign it to people.

> **Roles → Create Role → name it → Create Role**

:::info[Roles are the right way to grant access]
You *can* give a person permissions directly, but roles are what you should reach
for. One change to a role updates everyone who holds it; direct permissions have to
be fixed person by person. See
[Manage a User's Direct Permissions](/operator-administrator/manage-user-permissions).
:::

**Permission needed:** `operator.roles.write`

:::note
You need the permissions named above. The screenshots below use `https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Operator Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Operator Portal](/ferry-operator/sign-in).
- You hold `operator.roles.write`. Without it the **Create Role** button does not
  appear.
- You know roughly which permissions the role will need — though you assign those
  in the [next step](/operator-administrator/manage-role-permissions), not here.

## Step 1 — Open Roles

In the sidebar, **Roles** and **Users** are top-level items. Open **Roles** (route: `/roles`).

The list shows one row per role, with a **search** box and a **⋯** actions menu on
each row.

## Step 2 — Click Create Role

**Create Role** is at the top right. The **Add New Role** page opens with a single
form, headed **New Role** — _"Define the identity of this security role."_

## Step 3 — Name the role

| Field | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| **Role Name** | At least **2 characters**. Must be **unique**. Example placeholder: _e.g. Project Manager_. |

Then click **Create Role**. You are returned to the Roles list.

:::tip[Name it after the job, not the person]
_Boarding Gate Officer_ ages well; _Budi's role_ does not. The name is what the next
administrator reads when deciding who should hold it, and it is also what you must
type to [delete the role](/operator-administrator/edit-delete-role#delete-a-role) later.
:::

## If the save is rejected

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| _"Role name must be at least 2 characters."_ | The form's own check, before anything is sent. |
| _"Role name is required."_ | The name arrived empty. |
| _"Role name must be unique."_ | A role with that name already exists. Check the list — you may be about to duplicate one that already does the job. |

## What you have now — and what is missing

The role exists, but it is **empty**. A role with no permissions grants nothing, and
assigning it to someone changes nothing about what they can do.

Two steps remain:

1. [Give the role its permissions](/operator-administrator/manage-role-permissions).
2. [Assign the role to a user](/operator-administrator/assign-role).
