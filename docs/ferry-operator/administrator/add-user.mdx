Creating a portal user is one half of giving someone access. The other half is
**their own registration** — and the two are matched by **email address**.

> **Users → Create User → username + email → Create User**

**Permission needed:** `operator.users.write`

:::note
You need the permissions named above. The screenshots below use `https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## How the two accounts fit together

:::warning[Read this before you create anyone]
There are **two separate things** with the same person behind them:

| | Created by | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sign-in account** | The person, at [Register an Account](/register-account) | Email + password |
| **Portal user** | **You**, here | Username, roles, permissions, active flag |

They are linked **by email address, at sign-in time**. If the two emails differ by
so much as a typo, the person will confirm their email, you will activate their
user, and they will *still* be told their account is not provisioned — with nothing
obviously wrong on either side.

**Use the exact email address the person registered with.**
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in to the Operator Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Operator Portal](/ferry-operator/sign-in).
- You hold `operator.users.write`. Without it the **Create User** button does not
  appear.
- You know the **exact email address** the person has registered — or will register
  — with.

## Step 1 — Open Users

In the sidebar, **Roles** and **Users** are top-level items. Open **Users** (route: `/users`).

Each row shows the **username** with the **email** beneath it, and status badges:
**Active** / **Inactive**, plus **Admin** and **Super Admin** where they apply.

## Step 2 — Click Create User

**Create User** is at the top right. The **New User** form has exactly two fields.

## Step 3 — Fill in the two fields

| Field | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| **Username** | At least **3 characters**. Must be **unique**. This is the display name across the portal and the audit trail. |
| **Email Address** | Must be a valid email, and **must match the address on their sign-in account**. Must be unique. |

Then click **Create User**.

:::info[There is no Admin tick on this form]
Administrator status is **not** set at creation — the form is username and email
only. To make someone an admin, create them first, then use
[Grant or Revoke the Admin Role](/operator-administrator/grant-revoke-admin).
:::

## Step 4 — What happens next

On success you see a toast: **"User created — a notification email has been sent to
_[email]_."**

The person receives a **"You've been added to _[system name]_"** email containing a
link to the **registration** page, reminding them to register with that same address
if they have not already.

:::tip[The email is best-effort]
The user record is saved **first**, and the email is queued afterwards. If the mail
system is having a bad day, the **user is still created** — you just will not get
the toast's promise honoured. Tell the person directly rather than assuming the
email arrived.
:::

## Step 5 — Activate them

A new user **cannot sign in yet**. Two more things are needed:

1. **They register** (or have already registered) with the matching email and
   confirm it — see [Register an Account](/register-account).
2. **You activate them** — see
   [Activate or Deactivate a User](/operator-administrator/activate-deactivate-user).
   In the Operator Portal this needs a **Super Admin**, even if you created the user.

Then give them access:

3. [Assign a role](/operator-administrator/assign-role) — otherwise they can sign in and see
   almost nothing.

## If the save is rejected

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| _"Username is required."_ | The username arrived empty. |
| _"Username is already taken."_ | Another user has it. Usernames are unique. |
| _"Email is required."_ | The email arrived empty. |
| _"Email is already registered."_ | A portal user already exists with that address — search the Users list before creating a second one. |
| _"Invalid email address."_ | The form's own format check. |

## Troubleshooting: "not provisioned" after everything looks right

The person signs in and still sees _"Login succeeded, but your account is not
provisioned for Opusbay access. Please contact your administrator."_ Work through
these in order:

1. **Is the portal user Active?** An inactive user gets exactly this message.
2. **Do the emails match exactly?** Compare the address on their row here against
   the one they registered with. This is the most common cause.
3. **Have they confirmed their email?** Registration is not complete until they do.
4. **Are they in the right portal?** A Terminal Portal user cannot sign in to the
   Operator Portal, or vice versa — they need a user record in each.
