Ferry operators can add a passenger directly onto a departure trip. The passenger
is recorded against the trip, issued a boarding pass, and shown in the trip's
passenger list with status **CheckedIn**.

> **Departure Trips → Filter the trip → View → Check In Passenger → CheckedIn**

:::info[What operators can check in]
Through the Operator Portal, ferry operators check in **Ordinary** passengers
only. Check-in is available **only on upcoming trips that are still in
`Scheduled` status** — once a trip starts boarding (or is closed, departed, or
cancelled) it no longer accepts check-ins here — and the trip must have available
seats.
:::

:::note
You must be signed in and have an operator workspace selected — see
[Sign In to the Operator Portal](/ferry-operator/operator-sign-in). The
screenshots below use `https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and have selected your operator.
- The departure trip exists for the date you want.

## Step 1 — Open Departure Trips and filter to the trip

From the sidebar, open **Departure Trips**. The trips are shown as a grid of
days × departure times. Use **Show Filters** to narrow the results — for this
guide, set both **From Date** and **To Date** to **today** so only today's trips
remain.

![Departure Trips filtered to today](/img/ferry-operator/check-in-passenger/01-departure-trips.png)
_Departure Trips filtered to a single day. Each trip card has a **View** button._

## Step 2 — View the target trip

Find the trip you need (for this guide, the **last trip of the day**) and click
**View** on its card. The **Departure Trip Detail** page opens, showing the trip
info and a **Passengers** section with the **Check In Passenger** button.

![Departure Trip Detail with the Check In Passenger button](/img/ferry-operator/check-in-passenger/02-trip-detail.png)
_The trip detail page. Checked-in passengers appear in the Passengers table._

## Step 3 — Check in the passenger

Click **Check In Passenger** and complete the form:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **No** | Passport / ID number. |
| **Name** | Passenger's full name. |
| **Gender** | Optional; defaults to _Unspecified_. |
| **Nationality** | Required — pick from the country list. |
| **Date of Birth** | **Required** (`YYYY-MM-DD`). |
| **Expiry Date** | **Required** — travel-document expiry. |
| **Issue Date** / **Issue Country** | Optional travel-document details. |

Then click **Check In Passenger**.

![Check In Passenger dialog, completed](/img/ferry-operator/check-in-passenger/03-check-in-dialog.png)
_The Check In Passenger form. Date of Birth and Expiry Date are required._

## Outcome

The passenger is added to the trip as an **Ordinary** passenger and appears in
the **Passengers** table with:

- **Status: `Checked-In`** — from here the passenger progresses through
  **Pre-Immigration** and **Boarded** as they are scanned (or can be
  **Cancelled**).
- A unique **boarding pass number** — the passenger's reference for the rest of
  the boarding process, so make sure it's captured.

The trip's **Checked-In** count and **Load** increase accordingly.

:::tip
Use the **Passengers** search or **Show Filters** on the trip detail page to find
a specific passenger, and **Download Manifest** to export the full list.
:::
