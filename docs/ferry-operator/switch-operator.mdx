If you work for more than one ferry operator, **Switch** moves you between
workspaces without signing out and back in.

> **Switch (top right) → Select Operator → pick the workspace**

:::warning[Everything you see and do is scoped to the current workspace]
Trips, passengers, vessels, timeslots and reports all belong to the operator you
have selected. Checking a passenger in, or swapping a vessel, while the **wrong**
workspace is active affects that operator's data.

Check the **operator name and logo at the top left** before you act.
:::

## Step 1 — Check which operator you are in

The operator's **logo and name** sit at the top left of every page, next to the menu
button. That is your single source of truth for which workspace is active.

![The Operator Portal header with the operator name and the Switch button](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/04-my-operator.png)
_The active operator — **SINDO FERRY PTE LTD** — at the top left of the header, and
**Switch** at the top right._

## Step 2 — Click Switch

On a desktop screen, click **Switch** at the top right of the header.

On a narrow or mobile screen there is no **Switch** button — open the **account
avatar** menu at the top right and choose **Switch Operator** instead.

## Step 3 — Pick the workspace

You are returned to the **Select Operator** screen with your saved operators listed.

- **An operator you already use:** click its card, or find it with the **search**
  box.
- **A new operator:** type its **Operator Code** into **Enter operator code…** and
  click **+** to verify and add it.

![Select Operator screen with the Operator Code entered](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/02-select-operator.png)
_The Select Operator screen — pick a saved workspace, or add one by code._

You land on that operator's **Dashboard**, and the name at the top left updates.

## What switching does and does not do

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **You stay signed in** | Switching changes the workspace only. Your account session is untouched, so no password is needed. |
| **Your saved operators are kept** | The list persists on this browser. Clearing site data, or using a different computer or browser, means entering codes again. |
| **Unsaved work is lost** | An open dialog — a half-filled check-in form, for instance — is discarded. Finish or cancel it first. |
| **Permissions are per workspace** | You may have different permissions with different operators, so the sidebar can look different after switching. |

## Removing an operator from your list

On the **Select Operator** screen, each saved card has a **bin** icon. Removing a
workspace only takes it off this device's list — it does not touch your account or
the operator's data, and you can add it back with the code.

Worth doing when you stop working for an operator, so you cannot pick it by mistake.

## Signing out completely

**Switch Operator** does **not** sign you out. To end your session:

- From the **Select Operator** screen, click **Sign out of account** at the bottom;
  or
- From any page, open the **account avatar** menu at the top right and choose the
  sign-out option.

:::tip[On a shared counter machine, sign out — don't just switch]
Switching leaves your account signed in, so the next person can reach every
workspace you have saved. At the end of a shift on a shared machine, sign out
properly.
:::
