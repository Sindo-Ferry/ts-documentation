Signing in has **two parts**: authenticate with your account, then choose the
**operator workspace** you are working in. Everything else in the portal — trips,
passengers, manifests — is scoped to that workspace, so getting it right matters.

> **Login → Sign In → Select Operator (Operator Code) → Dashboard**

:::note
Your **Operator Code** comes from your administrator. The screenshots below use an
example code and `https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- A registered account with a **confirmed email**, **activated** by an
  administrator — see [Register an Account](/register-account).
- Your **Operator Code**.

## Step 1 — Sign in with your account

Open the Operator Portal and go to the login page. Enter your registered **Email**
and **Password**, then click **Sign In**.

![Operator Portal sign-in page](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/01-login.png)
_The Operator Portal sign-in page._

## Step 2 — Select your operator

The **Select Operator** screen asks which workspace you are entering:

- **First time:** type your **Operator Code** into the **Enter operator code…**
  field and click the **+** button. The code is verified and the workspace is added
  to your list.
- **Returning:** your verified operators are saved below. Click the workspace card —
  or use the **search** box if you have several — to continue.

![Select Operator screen with the Operator Code entered](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/02-select-operator.png)
_Enter the Operator Code and add it, or pick a saved workspace._

:::tip[Saved workspaces stay on this device]
Once verified, an operator stays in your list so you do not need the code again.
Each card has a **bin** icon to remove it — useful if you no longer work for that
operator. Removing it does not affect your account; you can add it back with the
code.
:::

## Step 3 — You're in

You land on the **Dashboard** for that workspace, showing today's trips, boarding
activity and passenger totals. The **operator's name and logo** appear at the top
left — always check this matches who you are working for.

![Operator Portal dashboard](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/03-dashboard.png)
_The Operator Portal dashboard after selecting the operator._

The sidebar gives you the modules you have permission for:

| Menu | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The dashboard |
| **Vessels** | [Look up a vessel](/ferry-operator/look-up-vessel) — read-only |
| **Timeslots** | [Look up a timeslot](/ferry-operator/look-up-timeslot) — read-only |
| **Departure Trips** | [Look up a trip](/ferry-operator/look-up-trip), [change a vessel](/ferry-operator/change-trip-vessel), [check passengers in](/ferry-operator/check-in-passenger) |
| **Passengers** | [Look up a passenger](/ferry-operator/look-up-passenger) — read-only |
| **Operator** | Your operator's own details |
| **Reports** | [Export the Passenger Manifest report](/ferry-operator/export-manifest-report) |

:::info[You only ever see your own operator's data]
Every list in the portal is scoped to the workspace you selected — trips,
passengers, vessels, timeslots, and the trip pickers inside filters and reports.
Another ferry operator's records never appear. If you ever see one, stop and report
it to your administrator immediately.
:::

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _"Email not confirmed. Please check your inbox."_ | Your email address has not been confirmed. | Use the confirmation link, or **Resend confirmation email** if it expired — see [Register an Account](/register-account#step-2--confirm-your-email). |
| _"Login succeeded, but your account is not provisioned for Opusbay access. Please contact your administrator."_ | Your account exists but has not been activated. | Ask your administrator to activate it — see [Register an Account](/register-account#step-3--get-activated-by-an-administrator). |
| The Operator Code is not accepted | Wrong code, or it does not belong to you. | Check it with your administrator — codes are per operator. |
| A sidebar item you expect is missing | You do not hold the permission for that module. | Ask your administrator. |
