Signing in to the Operator Portal has two parts: authenticate with your account,
then choose your **operator workspace** using an **Operator Code**. Other
Operator guides link here instead of repeating this step.

> **Login → Sign In → Select Operator (enter Operator Code) → Dashboard**

:::note
Your **Operator Code** is provided by your administrator — enter the one for your
operator. The screenshots below use an example code and the local environment at
`http://localhost:5001`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- A Ferry Operator account and password (or a linked Google / Microsoft account).
- Your **Operator Code** (from your administrator).

## Step 1 — Sign in with your account

Open the Operator Portal and go to the login page. Enter your **Email** and
**Password**, then click **Sign In**. Single sign-on with **Google** or
**Microsoft** is also available.

![Operator Portal sign-in page](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/01-login.png)
_The Operator Portal sign-in page._

## Step 2 — Select your operator

After signing in, the **Select Operator** screen asks you to choose a workspace:

- **First time:** type your **Operator Code** (provided by your administrator)
  into the **Enter operator code…** field and click the **+** button to verify and
  add it.
- **Returning:** your verified operators are saved below — just click the
  workspace card (or search for it) to continue.

![Select Operator screen with the Operator Code entered](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/02-select-operator.png)
_Enter the Operator Code and add it, or pick a saved workspace._

## Step 3 — You're authenticated

Once the operator is selected, you land on the **Dashboard** for that workspace
(here, **Sindo Ferry Pte Ltd**), showing today's trips, boarding activity, and
passenger totals. Use the sidebar to reach **Departure Trips**, **Passengers**,
and the other modules.

![Operator Portal dashboard](/img/ferry-operator/sign-in/03-dashboard.png)
_The Operator Portal dashboard after selecting the operator._

:::tip
Use **Switch** (top right) at any time to change to a different operator
workspace, or **Sign out of account** on the Select Operator screen to log out
completely.
:::
