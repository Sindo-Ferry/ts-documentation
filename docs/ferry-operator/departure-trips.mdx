The **Departure Trips** page shows your operator's trips in a **weekly calendar**.
From here you can **reassign the vessel** on one or more trips, open a trip's
**detail**, and **download the manifest** for a departed trip.

> **Departure Trips → (select trips) → Assign Vessel → choose vessel → Confirm Assign**

:::note
The screenshots below use `https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to the Operator Portal — see
  [Sign In to the Operator Portal](/ferry-operator/operator-sign-in).

## The weekly calendar

Open **Departure Trips** (route: `/departure-trips`). It shows a grid of **departure
times** (rows) across the **dates** of a **one-week** window (today through the next
6 days) by default — adjust the **date range** and other filters at the top. Each
trip cell shows the trip code, route, assigned **vessel**, the seat load, and
status.

![The operator Departure Trips weekly calendar](/img/ferry-operator/departure-trips/01-calendar.png)
_The weekly calendar of departure trips._

### Compact view

To fit a whole week on screen, click **Compact view** (top right) — it shrinks the
cells. Click **Normal view** to switch back.

![The Departure Trips calendar in compact view](/img/ferry-operator/departure-trips/02-compact.png)
_Compact view fits more of the week on screen._

## Change the assigned vessel

You can reassign the vessel on **one or many** trips at once:

1. **Filter** the calendar down to the trips you want (by date range, route, status, etc.).
2. **Select the trips**:
   - **Click** a trip to select just that one.
   - **Ctrl + Click** (⌘ on Mac) to add / remove trips **one by one**.
   - **Shift + Click** to select a **block** (range) of trips from the first selected.
3. The bar at the bottom shows how many are selected — e.g. **"N trip cell(s)
   selected"**. Click **Assign Vessel**.
4. In the **Assign Vessel to Selected Trips** dialog, **choose a vessel**, then click
   **Confirm Assign**. This changes the vessel on **all selected trips**.

![Selected trips with the Assign Vessel bar, and the Assign Vessel dialog](/img/ferry-operator/departure-trips/03-assign.png)
_Select trips → Assign Vessel → choose a vessel → Confirm Assign._

:::warning[Check the selected count first]
The dialog states **"This will assign one vessel to N selected trip(s)."** Confirm
that **N** matches the number of trips you intended before clicking **Confirm
Assign** — the change applies to **every** selected trip.
:::

:::info[When a vessel change is allowed]
The backend enforces these rules on assignment:

- Only **Scheduled**, **Boarding**, or **Closed** trips can change vessel —
  **Departed** or **Cancelled** trips are rejected (and can't be selected).
- The chosen vessel must be **active**.
- The vessel's **capacity must be at least the seats already occupied** on each trip —
  a vessel that is too small for a trip's existing passengers is rejected.
:::

## View a trip and download the manifest

Each trip cell also has:

- **View** — opens the trip's **detail** page.
- **Manifest** — available once the trip is **Closed** or **Departed**; choose a
  language (**English** or **Indonesian**) to download the passenger manifest as an
  Excel file.
