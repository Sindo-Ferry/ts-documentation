When a vessel breaks down, comes off service, or is swapped at the last minute, you
reassign the affected trips here. You can do **one trip or a whole day** in a single
action.

> **Departure Trips → select the trips → Assign Vessel → choose a vessel → Confirm Assign**

:::note
You need the assign-vessel permission. Without it you can still select trips, but
the **Assign Vessel** button never appears. The screenshots below use
`https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Before you start

- You are signed in with the right operator selected — see
  [Sign In](/ferry-operator/sign-in).
- You know the **replacement vessel** is **Active** and has **enough capacity** —
  check it on [Look Up a Vessel](/ferry-operator/look-up-vessel). This is the single
  most common reason a swap is refused.

## Step 1 — Find the trips

Open **Departure Trips** and filter down to what you need — by date range, route,
vessel or status. Filtering by the **outgoing vessel** is the quickest way to find
every trip a broken-down boat was due to run.

Note the current vessel and seat load before you change anything.

![The Departure Trips grid with the Assign a vessel hint](/img/ferry-operator/check-in-passenger/01-departure-trips.png)
_Departure Trips. The hint above the grid — **Assign a vessel to trips** — is the
reminder of how selection works._

## Step 2 — Select the trips

Click a trip's cell to select it. It highlights, and a **dark bar appears at the
bottom** of the screen reading **"1 trip selected"** with a one-line summary of the
date, time and route.

To select more:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Click** | Select just that trip (replacing any existing selection) |
| **Ctrl + click** (**⌘ + click** on a Mac) | Add or remove one trip at a time |
| **Shift + click** | Select the whole run between the first selection and this one |
| **Click a date heading** (along the top) | Select every selectable trip on that date |
| **Click a departure-time heading** (down the left) | Select every selectable trip at that time, across all days shown |
| **Click the only selected trip again** | Deselect it; the bar disappears |
| **Clear** in the bottom bar | Empty the whole selection |

![Six trips selected, with the bar at the bottom of the screen](/img/ferry-operator/departure-trips/04-select-trip.png)
_Six trips selected (highlighted in green). The bar reads **6 trips selected** with
a summary — **30 Sep – 5 Oct · 2 times · HFC - WFC** — and offers **Clear** and
**Assign Vessel**._

:::info[Departed and cancelled trips cannot be selected]
Their cells are **not clickable** and never join a selection. Click a **date
heading** on a day that mixes statuses and only the **Scheduled, Boarding and
Closed** trips are selected — the count in the bar reflects that, so it may be lower
than the number of cells you can see.

This is the system protecting you: a sailing that has already gone cannot have its
vessel rewritten.
:::

## Step 3 — Assign the vessel

Click **Assign Vessel** in the bottom bar. The **Assign Vessel to Selected Trips**
dialog opens, stating: _"This will assign one vessel to **N** selected trip(s)."_

:::warning[Check N before you confirm]
**N** is the number of trips that will change. A stray Shift+click can select a
whole week. If N is not the number you intended, **Cancel** and re-select.
:::

![The Assign Vessel to Selected Trips dialog](/img/ferry-operator/departure-trips/03-assign.png)
_The dialog confirms how many trips will change. **Confirm Assign** stays greyed out
until a vessel is picked._

Click **Select Vessel**, pick from the list, then click **Confirm Assign** (or
**Cancel** to back out).

The dialog closes, the grid refreshes, and the trips show the new vessel.

:::tip[The picker only offers your own active vessels]
Another operator's vessel never appears, and neither does an inactive one.
:::

## Step 4 — Verify

Open one of the trips (**View**) and check:

- **Vessel** is the new one, with its `Cap.` value.
- **Load** and **Available** have been recalculated: available seats are the **new
  vessel's capacity minus the passengers already checked in**.

## When a swap is refused

Nothing is saved when a swap fails. The message tells you which rule bit:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _"Vessel **X** (Capacity: 100) is too small for trip **SSFWH0930** which already has 120 occupied seats."_ | The replacement cannot carry the passengers already checked in on that trip. The message names the vessel, its capacity, the trip and the seats taken. | Pick a bigger vessel, or move those passengers first. |
| _"The selected vessel is inactive. Reactivate it before assigning it to trips."_ | The vessel is not active. | Ask the terminal to reactivate it, or pick another. |
| _"Only Scheduled, Boarding, or Closed trips can change vessel. Invalid: **CODE1, CODE2**."_ | A departed or cancelled trip got into the selection. | Deselect those trips — the named codes tell you which. |
| _"One or more Departure Trip IDs are invalid."_ | The selection is stale — a trip changed underneath you. | **Refresh** the grid and re-select. |

:::danger[A batch is all-or-nothing]
If **one** trip in the selection fails, **none** of them change — not even the ones
that would have been fine. The whole assignment is saved together or not at all.

So after a refusal, check **every** trip you selected: they all still have their
original vessel. Fix the problem the message names, then run the whole batch again.
:::

## What a vessel change does not touch

Only the **vessel** and the resulting **seat capacity** change. Everything else
stays exactly as it was:

- trip **status**, and boarding continues uninterrupted on a Boarding trip;
- **departure and gate times**, including any recorded delay;
- the **gate and berth** assigned by the terminal;
- every **passenger**, their **status**, and their **boarding pass number** — passes
  do not need reissuing.

:::tip[Trips already on that vessel are left alone]
Selecting a mix where some trips already use vessel A and then assigning vessel A
succeeds — the ones already on it are untouched, the rest move across. No need to
pick your selection apart first.
:::

## This change is per trip, not permanent

Reassigning affects **only the trips you selected**. The
[timeslot's](/ferry-operator/look-up-timeslot) **Default Vessel** is unchanged, so
newly generated trips go back to the original vessel.

For a permanent change, ask the terminal to update the timeslot — otherwise you will
be repeating this every day.
