Once a passenger is checked in, operators can **edit** their details or
**cancel** (remove) their check-in directly from the passenger table on the
**Departure Trip Detail** page.

> **Passengers table → row Actions (…) → Edit Passenger _or_ Cancel Passenger**

:::note
You must be signed in with an operator selected — see
[Sign In to the Operator Portal](/ferry-operator/operator-sign-in). The
screenshots below use `https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/`.
:::

## Prerequisites

- You are viewing a trip's **Departure Trip Detail** page (open **Departure
  Trips → View** on a trip).
- The trip already has at least one checked-in passenger — see
  [Check In a Passenger](/ferry-operator/operator-check-in-passenger).

## Open the row Actions menu

In the **Passengers** table, click the **…** (Actions) button at the end of a
passenger's row. A menu appears with **View Passenger**, **Edit Passenger**, and
**Cancel Passenger**.

![Passenger row Actions menu](/img/ferry-operator/edit-remove-passenger/01-action-menu.png)
_The row Actions menu: View, Edit, and Cancel Passenger._

## Edit a passenger

1. In the Actions menu, click **Edit Passenger**.
2. The **Edit Passenger** dialog opens, pre-filled with the passenger's current
   details (No, Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Expiry Date, Nationality,
   Issue Country, Issue Date).
3. Change the fields you need to correct, then click **Save Changes** (or
   **Cancel** to discard).

![Edit Passenger dialog](/img/ferry-operator/edit-remove-passenger/02-edit-dialog.png)
_The Edit Passenger dialog, pre-filled with the passenger's details._

## Remove (cancel) a passenger

1. In the Actions menu, click **Cancel Passenger** (the red option).
2. A confirmation asks you to confirm — it names the passenger being cancelled.
   Click **Confirm Cancel** (or **Keep Passenger** to abort).

![Cancel Passenger confirmation](/img/ferry-operator/edit-remove-passenger/03-cancel-confirm.png)
_Confirm before cancelling the passenger's check-in._

:::info
**Cancel Passenger** cancels the check-in rather than deleting the record. The
passenger's **Status** changes to **`Cancelled`**, the seat is released (the
trip's **Checked-In** count drops and **Available** increases), and the row
remains in the table for reference.
:::

![Passenger shown as Cancelled after removal](/img/ferry-operator/edit-remove-passenger/04-cancelled-result.png)
_After confirming, the passenger's status shows **Cancelled** and the seat is freed._
