Both **terminal staff** and **ferry operators** create their account on the same
public registration page before they can sign in to either portal:

> **Register → Confirm your email → Administrator activates your account → Sign in**

:::note
The registration page is public — no sign-in required:
`https://ts-terminal.sindoferry.com.sg/public/register/`
:::

## Step 1 — Sign up

Open the registration page and fill in the form:

- **Full Name**
- **Email** — you will need access to this inbox to confirm it.
- **Password** / **Confirm Password**

Then click **Sign Up**.

![Create an Account registration form](/img/public/register.png)
_The public registration form._

## Step 2 — Confirm your email

After signing up, a confirmation email is sent to the address you registered.
Open it and click the **confirmation link** — you are redirected back to the
login page, ready to sign in.

:::tip[No email in your inbox?]
Check your **Spam / Junk** folder. If it is not there either, use the resend
flow below.
:::

### If the confirmation link expired (resend)

Confirmation links are time-limited. If yours expired — or the email never
arrived — simply try to **sign in** with your registered email and password. The
login page will show *"Email not confirmed. Please check your inbox."* together
with a **Resend confirmation email** button. Click it, then check your inbox
(and spam folder) for the new confirmation email.

![Login page showing the Resend confirmation email button](/img/public/resend-confirmation.png)
_Signing in with an unconfirmed email reveals the **Resend confirmation email** button._

## Step 3 — Get activated by an administrator

Confirming your email is not the final step: a new account must also be
**activated by an administrator** before it can access a portal. If you sign in
before activation, you will see:

> _"Login succeeded, but your account is not provisioned for Opusbay access.
> Please contact your administrator."_

If you see this message, contact your administrator and ask them to activate
your account.

### For administrators — creating and activating the portal user

Registering creates the **sign-in account only** — the new person does **not**
appear in any portal's **Users** list yet. To grant them access, work in the
portal they need (Terminal Portal for terminal staff, Operator Portal for
ferry-company staff):

1. **Create their portal user** — open **Users**, click **Create User**, and
   enter a username together with the **exact same email address** the person
   registered with. The person receives a notification email letting them know
   they've been added — it also reminds them to register with that same email
   if they haven't done so yet.
2. **Activate it** — back in the **Users** list, click the **… (Actions)** button
   on their row and choose **Activate**.

:::info[How the two accounts connect]
The sign-in account (email + password) and the portal user are matched by **email
address** at sign-in time. If the two emails differ, the person keeps seeing the
"not provisioned" message even after activation.
:::

Both steps can also be done **via API** — refer to the **API Documentation**
provided by the development team.

## Step 4 — Sign in

Once your email is confirmed and your account is activated, you can sign in:

- [Sign In to the Terminal Portal](/terminal-staff/sign-in) — terminal staff
- [Sign In to the Operator Portal](/ferry-operator/operator-sign-in) — ferry operators
