Sign In to the Operator Portal

Signing in has two parts: authenticate with your account, then choose the operator workspace you are working in. Everything else in the portal — trips, passengers, manifests — is scoped to that workspace, so getting it right matters.

Login → Sign In → Select Operator (Operator Code) → Dashboard

note

Your Operator Code comes from your administrator. The screenshots below use an example code and https://ts-operator.sindoferry.com.sg/.

Before you start​

  • A registered account with a confirmed email, activated by an administrator — see Register an Account.
  • Your Operator Code.

Step 1 — Sign in with your account​

Open the Operator Portal and go to the login page. Enter your registered Email and Password, then click Sign In.

The Operator Portal sign-in page.

Step 2 — Select your operator​

The Select Operator screen asks which workspace you are entering:

  • First time: type your Operator Code into the Enter operator code… field and click the + button. The code is verified and the workspace is added to your list.
  • Returning: your verified operators are saved below. Click the workspace card — or use the search box if you have several — to continue.

Enter the Operator Code and add it, or pick a saved workspace.

Saved workspaces stay on this device

Once verified, an operator stays in your list so you do not need the code again. Each card has a bin icon to remove it — useful if you no longer work for that operator. Removing it does not affect your account; you can add it back with the code.

Step 3 — You're in​

You land on the Dashboard for that workspace, showing today's trips, boarding activity and passenger totals. The operator's name and logo appear at the top left — always check this matches who you are working for.

The Operator Portal dashboard after selecting the operator.

The sidebar gives you the modules you have permission for:

MenuWhat it is for
OverviewThe dashboard
VesselsLook up a vessel — read-only
TimeslotsLook up a timeslot — read-only
Departure TripsLook up a trip, change a vessel, check passengers in
PassengersLook up a passenger — read-only
OperatorYour operator's own details
ReportsExport the Passenger Manifest report
You only ever see your own operator's data

Every list in the portal is scoped to the workspace you selected — trips, passengers, vessels, timeslots, and the trip pickers inside filters and reports. Another ferry operator's records never appear. If you ever see one, stop and report it to your administrator immediately.

Troubleshooting​

MessageWhat it meansWhat to do
"Email not confirmed. Please check your inbox."Your email address has not been confirmed.Use the confirmation link, or Resend confirmation email if it expired — see Register an Account.
"Login succeeded, but your account is not provisioned for Opusbay access. Please contact your administrator."Your account exists but has not been activated.Ask your administrator to activate it — see Register an Account.
The Operator Code is not acceptedWrong code, or it does not belong to you.Check it with your administrator — codes are per operator.
A sidebar item you expect is missingYou do not hold the permission for that module.Ask your administrator.
